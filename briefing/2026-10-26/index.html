Sanya's Haitang district brings elderly health education to residents' doorsteps

中央广网报道，三亚海棠区新时代文明实践中心在长海社区开展“科普巴士”活动，围绕合理饮食、规律作息、适度运动、高血压糖尿病预防、骨质疏松防护与居家防滑防摔，以展板讲解、手册解读和趣味问答把老年健康常识送到家门口；当地还常态化开展健康讲堂与义诊。

ENCNR reports that the Haitang district civilization practice center in Sanya held a "science-popularization bus" event in the Changhai community, covering balanced diets, regular routines, moderate exercise, hypertension and diabetes prevention, osteoporosis protection and fall prevention through exhibitions, handbook walkthroughs and quiz games. The district also runs regular health lectures, free clinics and "bench classrooms," bringing elderly health education directly into neighborhoods.
