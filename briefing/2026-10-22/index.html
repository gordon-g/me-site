"儿童肠菌移植技术联盟"在深圳成立，儿童精准营养与肠道微生态临床研究提速

Pediatric FMT technology alliance founded in Shenzhen to accelerate child gut-microbiota clinical research and precision nutrition

中儿童肠菌移植技术联盟成立暨学术论坛10月19日在深圳市儿童医院龙华院区举行，肠道微生态诊疗与临床研究中心同步挂牌，推动珠三角儿童肠菌移植规范化与多中心研究。专家梳理儿童益生菌科学选菌三原则：功效精确到株、足量与活性有保障、按年龄段安全合规，并强调本土循证证据是儿童精准营养的关键。

ENA pediatric fecal microbiota transplantation technology alliance was founded on October 19 at Shenzhen Children's Hospital Longgang campus, alongside a gut-microecology clinical research center, to standardize pediatric FMT and drive multicenter studies in the Greater Bay Area. Experts outlined three principles for strain-specific probiotic selection in children — evidence tied to named strains, guaranteed potency and viability, and age-appropriate safety compliance — stressing local-population evidence as key to pediatric precision nutrition.
