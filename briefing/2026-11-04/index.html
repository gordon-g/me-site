《细胞》：5981 株菌基因组揭示，工业化正驱动肠道菌趋同进化

Cell: 5,981 gut bacterial genomes reveal convergent evolution driven by industrialization

中德国基尔大学 Eric Alm 团队联合全球微生物组保护计划，从9国24地区434个物种水平基因组簇中培养测序5981株肠道细菌，发现工业化人群菌株蛋白组更大、泛基因组流动性更高，80%至90%的基因获得由水平基因转移驱动，维生素B12合成的cob与肽聚糖合成的mur操纵子出现跨物种趋同突变。

ENA team led by Eric Alm at Kiel University, working with the Global Microbiome Conservancy, cultured and sequenced 5,981 gut bacterial strains spanning 434 species-level clusters from 24 regions in nine countries. Strains from industrialized hosts carried larger proteomes and higher pangenome fluidity, with 80 to 90 percent of gene gains driven by horizontal gene transfer; the cob operon for vitamin B12 synthesis and the mur operon for peptidoglycan synthesis showed convergent non-synonymous mutations across species.

ENZhu Lan and Chen Rong of Peking Union Medical College Hospital, with BGI Life Science Research, published a multicentre prospective cohort in Nature Medicine on Nov 3 covering 356 perimenopausal women at 14 centres, with metagenomic sampling of four body sites before treatment and at weeks 4, 12 and 24. Oral estradiol plus dydrogesterone mainly shifted urogenital and tongue-coating microbiota while gut communities stayed stable; pre-treatment Lactobacillus crispatus abundance correlated with sexual function scores, women with disordered microbiota improved little, and the team built a machine-learning model to predict response.

700 例粪便宏基因组：肠道四界微生物可无创筛查胆管癌，平均 AUC 达 91%

Faecal metagenomics across four microbial kingdoms enables non-invasive cholangiocarcinoma screening

中郑大一附院任志刚团队与浙大一院郑树森团队对700例多中心粪便样本做宏基因组测序，同时解析古菌、细菌、真菌、病毒四界及功能特征，用梯度提升机整合21个标志物建模。模型在三个独立队列平均AUC达91%，鉴别胆管癌与肝细胞癌AUC为99.74%，成果发表于 Genome Medicine。

ENRen Zhigang of the First Affiliated Hospital of Zhengzhou University and Zheng Shusen of the First Affiliated Hospital of Zhejiang University sequenced faecal metagenomes from 700 multicentre samples, profiling archaea, bacteria, fungi and viruses plus functional features, then built a gradient-boosting model on 21 markers. The model reached a mean AUC of 91 percent across three independent cohorts, 99.74 percent for separating cholangiocarcinoma from hepatocellular carcinoma, and detected 97.6 percent of CA19-9-negative cases; the work appeared in Genome Medicine.

《细胞》：补充 DGLA 把衰老细胞推向铁死亡，开辟高选择性 senolytic 新路径

Cell: supplementing DGLA pushes senescent cells into ferroptosis, a selective senolytic route

中塔夫茨大学团队在 Cell 发文，发现多不饱和脂肪酸去饱和代谢是细胞在衰老与铁死亡间的分子开关：衰老细胞上调 FADS1/FADS2 比例、加速消耗 DGLA 以逃避铁死亡；外源 DGLA 经 CD36 摄入并引发膜脂过氧化，选择性杀伤衰老细胞，老龄小鼠口服后 p16 与 SASP 下降。

ENA Tufts University team reported in Cell that polyunsaturated fatty acid desaturation acts as the molecular switch deciding whether damaged cells senesce or undergo ferroptosis. Senescent cells raise the FADS1 to FADS2 ratio and shunt DGLA toward arachidonic acid, escaping ferroptosis; exogenous DGLA at 200 micromolar enters via CD36 and, with ALOX15-catalysed lipid peroxidation, selectively kills several senescent cell types. Five days of oral DGLA ethyl ester in old mice lowered p16 signalling, SASP expression and liver and kidney fibrosis after two weeks.

Castration in adulthood extends male mouse lifespan by about 33 percent

中得州大学圣安东尼奥健康科学中心 Nelson 团队在 bioRxiv 预印本报告：同品系雄鼠在青春期前、后与240日龄（约人类30岁）去势，寿命分别延长19%、26%与33%，成年期获益最大；但各组握力下降速度与对照组相同，提示去势延寿并非简单减缓衰老。研究者强调仅为小鼠结果且未经同行评审。

ENNelson and colleagues at UT Health San Antonio reported in a bioRxiv preprint that male mice of the same strain castrated before puberty, just after puberty, or at 240 days of age (roughly 30 human years) lived 19, 26 and 33 percent longer respectively, with the adult group gaining most. Grip strength and other functional decline progressed at the same rate as controls, suggesting the benefit is not simple slowing of ageing; the authors stress this is mouse data, not peer reviewed, and not transferable to humans.

西藏比如县启动慢病双管行动：管体重、管意识，建筛查到保障全链条

Tibet's Biru county launches chronic disease drive targeting weight and health literacy

中科学网11月3日报道，西藏那曲市比如县启动健康比如·健康共富双管行动暨十五五慢性病综合防控治行动，以管慢病体重、管健康意识为切口，依托县医疗集团构建筛查、干预、管理、保障全链条，衔接县乡村三级随访与转诊，并组织为期两天半的慢病防治能力培训，推动高原慢病防控由阶段性活动转向常态化。

ENScienceNet reported on Nov 3 that Biru county in Nagqu, Tibet, launched a dual-track chronic disease campaign for the 15th five-year plan period, targeting chronic disease weight control and health literacy. Relying on the county medical group, it builds a chain from screening and intervention to management and financing, links county, township and village follow-up and referral, and ran a two-and-a-half-day training course so high-altitude chronic disease control becomes routine rather than episodic.
