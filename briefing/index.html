ENChinese media reported on Oct 8 that a 42-year-old woman suffered bowel perforation and diffuse peritonitis after a high-end colonic detox package at a beauty salon and spent time in intensive care. Colorectal surgeons stress that enemas are medical procedures with strict requirements for fluid temperature, volume and pressure, that frequent enemas damage the gut microbiota and the natural defecation reflex, and that healthy people do not need colon cleansing for detox.
