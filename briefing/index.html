中南京晨报报道，秦淮区秦虹社区卫生服务中心打造健康体重管理门诊，已有 680 名居民签约，其中慢病患者占 91%，居民体重、内脏脂肪与糖脂指标稳步改善，辖区慢病新发增长放缓。中心以筛查同步宣教、流程简化、上门随访、多网点就近服务提升可及性，下一步推进全域筛查与老年肥胖合并肌少症管理。

ENNanjing Morning Post reports the Qinhong community health centre in Qinhuai district has enrolled 680 residents in its weight management package, 91 percent with chronic disease, with steady gains in weight, visceral fat and glycaemic and lipid markers and slower growth in new chronic cases locally. Screening paired with education, simplified processes, home visits and satellite sites drive uptake; next come district-wide screening and dedicated care for obesity with sarcopenia.
