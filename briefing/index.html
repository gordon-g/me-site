辉瑞计划两年内助建 500 家医院体重管理门诊，重点下沉县域

Pfizer to help build weight-management clinics in 500 hospitals over two years, focused on county-level care

中辉瑞中国计划 2026 年下半年至 2027 年在全国协助落实 500 家医院体重健康管理门诊，并重点打造 100 家标杆。其广阔市场事业部观察到，一线城市减重多指向外形与幸福感，县域人群则多在出现功能性障碍后才就诊，存在明显时间差；企业将配合主管部门为县域医护提供系统培训。

ENPfizer China plans to help set up weight-management clinics in 500 hospitals between late 2026 and 2027, with 100 developed as benchmarks. Its broader-market unit notes that in tier-1 cities weight loss is driven by appearance and wellbeing, while county patients usually present only after functional problems appear, a dangerous time lag. Training for county clinicians is part of the plan.
