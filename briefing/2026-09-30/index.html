Nature 背靠背两篇：幼年蛋白质限制通过储存蛋白 Lsp2「写入」成年寿命

Two back-to-back Nature papers: early-life protein restriction writes adult lifespan through storage protein Lsp2

中东京大学与湖南师范大学两团队 9 月 23 日在《Nature》背靠背发表果蝇研究，锁定储存蛋白 Lsp2 为「营养记忆」分子。幼虫期限制蛋白质可持续压低 Lsp2，进而减少成年早期核糖体蛋白供给、抑制翻译活性，最终延长寿命；直接沉默 Lsp2 可复现延寿效果，且不损害生殖与体型。研究还发现仅限制苯丙氨酸、酪氨酸等 Lsp2 富集氨基酸即足以延寿，为生命早期营养编程提供了分子层面的解释。

ENTwo teams — the University of Tokyo and Hunan Normal University — published back-to-back Drosophila studies in Nature on Sept 23 identifying the storage protein Lsp2 as a molecular carrier of nutritional memory. Larval protein restriction persistently lowers Lsp2, reducing early-adult ribosomal protein supply and translational activity, ultimately extending lifespan; silencing Lsp2 reproduces the effect without harming reproduction or body size. Restricting only Lsp2-enriched amino acids such as phenylalanine and tyrosine was sufficient to extend lifespan, offering a molecular account of early-life nutritional programming.

Nature Communications：AI 构建膳食指数 EDISEA，高分者表观遗传年龄年轻约 4 岁

Nature Communications: AI-derived EDISEA dietary index linked to ~4 years younger epigenetic age

中浙江大学医学院附属第一医院与公共卫生学院团队在《Nature Communications》发表研究，用弹性网络回归将 38 类食物摄入与 GrimAge2 加速数据关联，构建出「经验性减缓表观遗传衰老膳食指数」（EDISEA）。开发与验证队列中，最高与最低五分位组的 GrimAge2 加速分别相差 4.17 年与 3.77 年；高分组全因死亡风险合并下降（风险比 0.80），并与癌症、痴呆、心血管病及功能受限发生率下降相关。研究属观察性设计，尚不能证明因果。

ENResearchers from Zhejiang University's First Affiliated Hospital and School of Public Health used elastic net regression to link 38 food groups to GrimAge2 acceleration, constructing an Empirical Dietary Index for Slowing Epigenetic Aging (EDISEA). Across development and validation cohorts, the highest versus lowest quintile differed by 4.17 and 3.77 years of GrimAge2 acceleration respectively; higher scores were associated with lower all-cause mortality (pooled HR 0.80) and lower incidence of cancer, dementia, cardiovascular disease and functional limitation. The design is observational and cannot establish causation.

Nature Health：高纤维、多植化素饮食与更深睡眠及更低夜间心率相关

Nature Health: fiber- and phytochemical-rich diets linked to deeper sleep and lower nighttime heart rate

中9 月 27 日《Nature Health》发表基于 Human Phenotype Project（已入组 2.8 万人）的研究，分析 32 项膳食因素，识别出 7 项与睡眠结构改善相关，包括植物性食物种类、膳食纤维密度与植化素摄入。纤维摄入更高者深睡与 REM 睡眠增加；植物性食物多样性更高者夜间心率更低。研究同时发现晚餐吃得更丰盛者总睡眠时间略长，但夜间心率升高，提示延长的睡眠可能以睡眠质量为代价。

ENPublished Sept 27 in Nature Health, an analysis of 32 dietary factors in the Human Phenotype Project (28,000 enrolled) identified seven associated with better sleep architecture, including plant food variety, fiber density and phytochemical intake. Higher fiber intake tracked with more deep and REM sleep; greater minimally processed plant food variety tracked with lower nighttime heart rate. Heavier evening meals were linked to slightly longer total sleep but elevated nighttime heart rate, suggesting the extra sleep may come at a cost to quality.

耶鲁 Nature Medicine：51 项干预研究横评，生活方式与三类处方药可降低表观遗传年龄，非处方补充剂普遍无效

中耶鲁大学 Higgins-Chen 实验室汇总 51 项人体干预研究、110 余项 DNA 甲基化标志物（含 16 种主流表观遗传时钟）进行系统评估。结果显示：运动联合健康饮食（地中海、低碳或低脂均可）稳定降低表观遗传年龄；处方药降幅最大，以二甲双胍、司美格鲁肽与抗 TNF 疗法最为显著；而鱼油、NMN 等非处方补充剂及高压氧等医疗操作普遍未见显著效果。研究还提示新一代时钟（DunedinPACE、PCGrimAge）对干预更敏感，且患病受试者的标志物改变幅度大于健康人。

ENThe Higgins-Chen lab at Yale pooled 51 human intervention studies and more than 110 DNA methylation markers (including 16 major epigenetic clocks). Exercise combined with a healthy diet — Mediterranean, low-carb or low-fat — consistently lowered epigenetic age; prescription drugs produced the largest reductions, led by metformin, semaglutide and anti-TNF therapy; over-the-counter supplements such as fish oil and NMN, along with procedures like hyperbaric oxygen, generally showed no significant effect. Newer clocks (DunedinPACE, PCGrimAge) were more responsive, and marker changes were larger in diseased than healthy participants.

中大阪大学团队在《American Journal of Kidney Diseases》发表随机对照试验，对 36 例晚期慢性肾病患者比较含 70% 与 50% 植物蛋白的等量常蛋白饮食（0.8 g/kg/天）干预 4 周的代谢效应。70% 植物蛋白组血清 TMAO 显著下降（组间差 −4.6 μg/mL），碳酸氢盐与血钙升高、全段甲状旁腺激素下降，产短链脂肪酸菌属如 Butyricicoccus 增加、促炎菌属 Bilophila 减少。两组血钾与瘦体重无差异，提示未增加高钾血症风险。样本量较小，需更大规模验证。

ENPublished in the American Journal of Kidney Diseases, an Osaka University randomized trial compared 70% versus 50% plant-protein isonitrogenous diets (0.8 g/kg/day) over four weeks in 36 patients with advanced CKD. The 70% group showed a significant fall in serum TMAO (between-group difference −4.6 μg/mL), higher bicarbonate and serum calcium, lower intact parathyroid hormone, and shifts toward short-chain fatty acid producers such as Butyricicoccus with fewer pro-inflammatory Bilophila. Potassium and lean mass did not differ, suggesting no added hyperkalemia risk. The sample was small and needs larger validation.

美国国家衰老研究所启动尿石素 A 糖代谢三盲随机试验，计划入组 180 人

US National Institute on Aging launches triple-masked randomized trial of urolithin A for glucose metabolism, targeting 180 participants

中美国国家衰老研究所（NIA）登记的 NCT06274749 试验于 2026 年 9 月 30 日启动，计划入组 180 名 55 岁以上、BMI ≥ 27 的健康成人，随机接受每日 1 g 尿石素 A 或安慰剂共 8 周，采用四盲设计（受试者、照护者、研究者、结局评估者）。主要终点为补充后 β 细胞对葡萄糖的响应速率；次要终点包括肠促胰素分泌、基于大腿 MRI/MRS 的线粒体呼吸、脑氧化代谢、肠道菌群组成与免疫细胞比例。所用原料已获 FDA GRAS 认定（GRN No. 000791），预计 2027 年 3 月完成。

ENRegistered by the US National Institute on Aging, trial NCT06274749 starts Sept 30, 2026, aiming to enroll 180 healthy adults aged 55+ with BMI ≥ 27, randomized to 1 g urolithin A daily or placebo for eight weeks in a quadruple-masked design covering participants, care providers, investigators and outcome assessors. The primary endpoint is the rate at which beta cells respond to glucose; secondary endpoints include incretin secretion, mitochondrial respiration via thigh MRI/MRS, brain oxidative metabolism, gut microbiome composition and immune cell proportions. The material holds FDA GRAS status (GRN No. 000791), with completion expected in March 2027.

中国疾控中心：慢性病死亡占比超 88%，重在「主动健康、自我管理」

China CDC: chronic diseases account for over 88% of deaths, making proactive self-management essential

中今年全民健康文明生活方式宣传月主题为「主动健康 自我管理」。中国疾控中心慢病处处长赖建强、慢病中心主任吴静等专家指出，我国慢性病导致的死亡占总死亡的 88% 以上，仅靠患病后就医的被动模式难以遏制高发态势，必须推动健康关口前移。今年 7 月国务院印发《国民健康「十五五」规划》，提出到 2030 年人均预期寿命达 80 岁、居民健康素养水平提升至 40%。专家建议普通人优先做好几件关键事：定期体检并关注血压、血糖、血脂、腰围与体重；均衡膳食、避免久坐；不吸烟、限制饮酒、保证充足睡眠；指标异常及时就医。

ENThis year's national healthy-lifestyle campaign is themed 'proactive health, self-management.' Officials from China CDC's chronic disease division note that chronic diseases account for more than 88% of all deaths in China, and that a passive model of seeking care only after illness cannot curb the trend — health must be moved upstream. The State Council's 15th Five-Year National Health Plan, issued in July, targets average life expectancy of 80 years and health literacy of 40% by 2030. Experts advise prioritizing regular checkups tracking blood pressure, glucose, lipids, waist circumference and weight; balanced diet and less sitting; no smoking, limited alcohol and adequate sleep; and prompt care when markers go abnormal.

我国已启动新版《中国居民膳食指南》修订，将更强调个性化与中国特色

China has begun revising its national dietary guidelines, with stronger emphasis on personalization and Chinese dietary patterns

中国家卫生健康委表示将进一步组织做好《中国居民膳食指南》修订工作。现行指南除面向健康成人提出八条准则外，还制定 9 种特定人群膳食指南，并针对糖尿病、高血压等慢性病制定食养指南，提供不同地区、季节与饮食习惯的个性化食谱示例；同时依托数字化平台上线小程序端口提供膳食调理参考。修订方向包括：针对营养失衡制定符合国情的干预建议；用更通俗语言提升可操作性；结合科学前沿、国民营养监测数据与中医药传统智慧打造中国特色膳食模式；按地域与个人营养状况提出个性化建议。

ENChina's National Health Commission says it will further organize revision of the Chinese Dietary Guidelines. The current edition sets eight principles for healthy adults plus dietary guidelines for nine specific populations, alongside therapeutic diet guides for diabetes, hypertension and other chronic diseases with personalized menu examples by region, season and dietary habit; a digital platform and mini-program provide dietary reference tools. Revision directions include intervention advice tailored to national nutritional imbalances, more accessible and actionable language, a distinctly Chinese dietary pattern drawing on scientific frontiers, national nutrition surveillance data and traditional Chinese medicine wisdom, and personalized recommendations by region and individual status.

武汉：家庭医生按季监测血压血糖 BMI 腰围，完善「防筛治管康」闭环

Wuhan: family doctors to monitor blood pressure, glucose, BMI and waist quarterly, closing the prevention-to-rehabilitation loop

中武汉市卫健委在 9 月 29 日发布的政协提案答复中介绍，该市依托基本公共卫生服务、「323」攻坚行动、体重管理年活动与老年人体检等工作，为高血压、糖尿病等慢性病患者及高危人群建立健康档案，签约家庭医生每季度监测血压、血糖、BMI 与腰围，并根据监测结果开展营养配餐与科学运动宣教，落实个性化健康干预。2024–2025 年该市还组织覆盖 16 个区、4000 余人次的含糖加工食品消费量专项调查，为制定控糖干预策略提供数据支撑。

ENIn a Sept 29 reply to a political consultative conference proposal, the Wuhan Municipal Health Commission described building health records for patients with hypertension, diabetes and other chronic diseases as well as high-risk groups, drawing on basic public health services, the '323' campaign, the weight-management year and elderly checkups. Contracted family doctors monitor blood pressure, glucose, BMI and waist circumference quarterly and deliver nutrition and exercise guidance based on results. In 2024–2025 the city also ran a survey of sugar-sweetened processed food intake covering more than 4,000 person-visits across 16 districts to inform sugar-control strategy.

48 位专家共识发布：慢性病早期预防「十保、九减、八防」行动框架

Consensus from 48 experts: a 10-keep, 9-reduce, 8-prevent framework for early chronic disease prevention

中由「癌症、心脑血管、呼吸和代谢性疾病防治研究国家科技重大专项」项目组牵头、全国 48 位多学科专家共同制定的《面向个体健康行为促进的慢性病早期预防专家共识》在《中国慢性病预防与控制》杂志发表。共识提出「十保、九减、八防」框架：保持合理饮食、充足饮水（成人每日 1500–1700 毫升）、每周至少 150 分钟中等强度运动、良好睡眠（7–8 小时）与适宜 BMI（18.5–23.9 kg/m²）等十项；减少盐（＜5 克/天）、添加糖（＜25 克/天）、烹调油（25–30 克/天）、酒精、久坐等九类风险因素；防跌倒、防吸烟、防合餐、防极端天气等八项侵害，为成年居民提供系统化、普适化的早期预防行为框架。

ENLed by the national science and technology major project on cancer, cardiovascular, respiratory and metabolic disease prevention and developed by 48 multidisciplinary experts, a consensus on early chronic disease prevention through individual health behavior was published in the Chinese Journal of Prevention and Control of Chronic Diseases. It proposes a 10-keep, 9-reduce, 8-prevent framework: keep a balanced diet, adequate hydration (1,500–1,700 ml daily for adults), at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity weekly, good sleep (7–8 hours) and a BMI of 18.5–23.9 kg/m², among ten items; reduce nine risk factors including salt (under 5 g/day), added sugar (under 25 g/day), cooking oil (25–30 g/day), alcohol and prolonged sitting; and prevent eight harms including falls, smoking, shared utensil use and extreme weather.
