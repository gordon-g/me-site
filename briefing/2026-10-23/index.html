中重庆九龙坡区整合老年人历年体检、门诊与慢病随访数据，用 AI 生成个人健康画像，自动识别血压血糖控制不佳、血脂异常等 11 项风险并生成饮食、运动、复查、用药提醒等个性化方案，健康画像与绿通转诊让医养转介平均控制在 10 分钟内。全区 16.67 万老年人基础电子档案覆盖率达 87.98%，高血压、糖尿病管理率均近 88%，推动健康管理从“治已病”前移至“防未病”。

ENJiulongpo district in Chongqing integrates years of checkups, clinic records and chronic-disease follow-ups into AI-generated health portraits for seniors, automatically flagging 11 risks such as poor blood pressure and glucose control and producing personalized diet, exercise and medication plans, while fast-track referrals cut medical-elderly-care transfers to under 10 minutes. Basic electronic records now cover 87.98% of 166,700 seniors, shifting care from treatment toward prevention.

AI 癌症筛查走向综合健康风险评估：通用技术健管公司谈落地路径

From Lesion Detection to Whole-Person Risk: AI Cancer Screening's Next Step, Says Genertec Health

中2026 财富中国 500 强论坛上，通用技术健管公司董事长彭佳虹表示，AI 在影像筛查中能更快识别微小病变、降低漏诊，但本质仍是辅助工具，行业尚无完全独立于传统检查的筛查路径；公司正探索把血液、影像与生活方式等全周期数据整合进数字风险评估模型，实现从“单点检测”向“综合评估”升级，并随服务反馈持续迭代。

ENAt the 2026 Fortune China 500 Forum, Genertec Health Management chairwoman Peng Jiahong said AI can spot subtle lesions faster and reduce missed reads in imaging, but remains an assistive tool with no screening path fully independent of conventional exams. The company is integrating blood, imaging and lifestyle data into a whole-cycle digital risk-assessment model, moving from single-point detection to comprehensive evaluation with continuous iteration.
