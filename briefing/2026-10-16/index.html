中ScienceDaily报道的新研究解释了为何冰浴、按摩、压缩服等流行的运动恢复手段可能适得其反：运动后的炎症信号本身是肌肉适应与增强的必要刺激，过度抑制这些信号反而削弱训练效果。研究提示恢复策略应与训练目标匹配——追求表现提升时应"保留"适度炎症，仅在与训练负荷匹配的场景下使用恢复干预，为科学健身管理提供了新依据。

ENNew research covered by ScienceDaily explains why popular recovery tools such as ice baths, massage and compression garments can backfire: post-exercise inflammatory signaling is a necessary stimulus for muscle adaptation and strengthening, and suppressing it too aggressively blunts training gains. Recovery strategies should match training goals—preserving moderate inflammation when adaptation is the aim—which offers fresh guidance for evidence-based exercise management.
