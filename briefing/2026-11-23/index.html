停药数年仍留“菌群指纹”：2500余人研究揭示常用药物长期重塑肠道

Microbial fingerprints persist years after stopping common drugs, 2,500-person study finds

中爱沙尼亚塔尔图大学团队分析生物样本库2500余人的粪便与处方记录，发现抗抑郁药、β受体阻滞剂、质子泵抑制剂与苯二氮䓬类均与菌群改变相关，相当一部分在停药数年后仍可检出“微生物指纹”。同属苯二氮䓬的地西泮与阿普唑仑扰动强度并不相同，该类药物的影响量级可与广谱抗生素相比。研究者提醒，解读菌群数据必须纳入既往用药史。

ENResearchers at the University of Tartu analysed stool samples and prescription histories from more than 2,500 Estonian Biobank participants. Antidepressants, beta-blockers, proton pump inhibitors and benzodiazepines were all linked to microbiome changes, and many left detectable microbial fingerprints years after patients stopped taking them. Diazepam and alprazolam, both benzodiazepines, differed in how strongly they disrupted bacteria, and the class as a whole rivaled broad-spectrum antibiotics. The team urges clinicians to factor past prescriptions into microbiome interpretation.

中兰州大学生命科学学院李祥锴团队联合兰大二院，从西北传统发酵食品“浆水”中分离出植物乳杆菌GR-4，在荷瘤小鼠中观察到：单用GR-4抑瘤有限，单用PD-1抑制剂虽抑瘤却明显掉体重，两者联用对肿瘤控制最佳且显著缓解消耗。成果发表于Frontiers in Immunology，首次证实该菌与PD-1的协同价值，但团队强调仍处基础研究阶段。

ENA team led by Li Xiangkai at Lanzhou University, working with the university's second hospital, isolated Lactobacillus plantarum GR-4 from jiangshui, a traditional fermented food from northwest China. In tumour-bearing mice, GR-4 alone suppressed tumours only modestly, and PD-1 blockade alone shrank tumours but caused marked weight loss; the combination produced the best tumour control while easing wasting. Published in Frontiers in Immunology, the work is the first to show synergy between this strain and PD-1 inhibitors, though the team stresses it remains preclinical.

乙酸盐当“指挥官”：经FFAR2指令中性粒细胞分泌IL-22减轻结肠炎

Acetate directs neutrophils via FFAR2 to release IL-22 and dampen colitis

中坎皮纳斯州立大学与圣路易斯华盛顿大学团队在PNAS报告：膳食纤维发酵产生的乙酸，经中性粒细胞表面FFAR2受体指令其生产修复性细胞因子IL-22，从而缓解小鼠DSS结肠炎。反直觉的是，乙酸干预后结肠中性粒细胞数量不降反升；一旦清除中性粒细胞，乙酸的护肠作用完全消失。流式分析显示约20%的IL-22阳性细胞竟是中性粒细胞，且在ILC3缺陷小鼠中保护依旧。

ENTeams at UNICAMP and Washington University in St. Louis reported in PNAS that acetate, produced when gut bacteria ferment dietary fibre, instructs neutrophils through the FFAR2 receptor to make the repair cytokine IL-22, easing DSS colitis in mice. Counter-intuitively, neutrophil numbers in the colon rose after acetate treatment; depleting neutrophils abolished all protection. Flow analysis showed about 20 percent of IL-22-positive cells were neutrophils, and protection persisted in mice lacking ILC3s, marking an independent, non-redundant role.

丁酸梭菌载双药：先护脑再护肠，同步缓解肠炎与焦虑抑郁

Dual-drug Clostridium butyricum delivery eases colitis and anxiety together in mice

中郑州大学王蕾团队构建序贯递送系统：以丁酸梭菌为底盘，表面载5-氨基水杨酸，外层裹含吲哚-3-乙酸（IAA）的肠溶聚合物。小肠pH升至5.5以上时外壳先溶，IAA入血靶向大脑、恢复海马α7nAChR与胆碱能抗炎通路；核心抵达结肠后再释放5-ASA。切断膈下迷走神经后IAA的护肠效应几乎消失，提示其经迷走神经远程调控肠道炎症。

ENWang Lei's group at Zhengzhou University built a sequential delivery system using Clostridium butyricum as the chassis, loaded on its surface with 5-aminosalicylic acid and wrapped in an enteric polymer containing indole-3-acetic acid (IAA). Above pH 5.5 in the small intestine the shell dissolves first, releasing IAA into the blood to act on the brain, restoring hippocampal alpha7nAChR and the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway; the core then reaches the colon and releases 5-ASA. Cutting the subdiaphragmatic vagus abolished IAA's gut protection, showing remote control via the nerve.

阿尔茨海默“清理系统”被找到缺口：CASS4基因失灵让斑块失控扩散

Alzheimer's cleanup flaw traced to CASS4 gene in microglia

中弗吉尼亚大学团队在Journal of Experimental Medicine报告，长期与阿尔茨海默病风险相关的CASS4基因主要作用于小胶质细胞而非神经元。基因功能受损时，小胶质细胞包裹、压实β淀粉样斑块的能力下降，斑块更快扩散，邻近神经元受损、认知恶化。研究者提出，CASS4活性缺陷或可作早期生物标志物，恢复其功能可能成为新治疗方向，但现有证据来自小鼠。

ENResearchers at UVA Health reported in the Journal of Experimental Medicine that CASS4, a gene repeatedly linked to Alzheimer's risk, acts mainly in microglia rather than neurons. When the gene was disrupted in mice, microglia lost the ability to surround and compact amyloid-beta plaques, allowing them to spread faster, damaging nearby neurons and worsening cognition. The team suggests defective CASS4 activity could serve as an early biomarker and that boosting its function may offer a therapeutic route, though the evidence remains mouse-based.

一片降压药激活棕色脂肪：氯沙坦另辟代谢通路，10万人影像牵连情绪

Losartan switches on brown fat through a pathway separate from blood pressure

中洛克菲勒大学团队在Nature发表：常用降压药氯沙坦可在小鼠中激活棕色脂肪，提高耗氧与能量消耗而体重稳定，且激活通路与降压通路彼此独立，理论上可拆分开发。团队另分析逾10万名患者PET/CT，发现可检出棕色脂肪者血糖血脂、心功能与炎症指标更好，焦虑抑郁比例更低，但骨质疏松与部分皮肤病比例更高，SSRI则似抑制棕色脂肪。

ENA Rockefeller University team reported in Nature that losartan, a widely used blood pressure drug, activates brown fat in mice, raising oxygen consumption and energy expenditure while body weight stayed stable. Crucially, the fat-activating pathway is distinct from the blood-pressure one, suggesting the effects could be separated in future drugs. Analysing PET/CT from over 100,000 patients, the team found those with detectable brown fat had better glycaemic, lipid, cardiac and inflammatory profiles and lower rates of anxiety and depression, but higher rates of osteoporosis and some skin conditions; SSRIs appeared to suppress brown fat.

中一篇发表于Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy、45页548篇文献的综述把抗衰老干预分为三派：直接清除衰老细胞、抑制其SASP炎症分泌、把衰老细胞逆转回年轻状态。三者各有硬伤——清除易误伤正常细胞，平抑停药易反弹，逆转存在致瘤风险，作者主张以纳米递送精准投喂并联合用药。文中同时点出通讯作者与营养补充剂企业存在利益关联，尿石素A、麦角硫因等结论证据强度需打折。

ENA 45-page review with 548 references in Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy groups anti-aging interventions into three camps: clearing senescent cells outright, suppressing their inflammatory SASP secretion, and reprogramming them back toward youth. Each has a catch: clearance can hit healthy cells, suppression rebounds after stopping, and reprogramming carries tumorigenic risk, so the authors argue for nanoparticle-guided delivery and combination regimens. ENA medRxiv cohort study linked 2013 dietary data to 2016 DNA methylation measures in 1,740 participants of the US Health and Retirement Study. Each one-standard-deviation higher MIND diet score was associated with 0.4 fewer years of PhenoAge acceleration (95% CI -0.8 to -0.1), 0.3 fewer years of GrimAge acceleration, and a slower methylation pace of aging, with associations persisting after BMI adjustment and selection weighting. The design is observational, so causality is unproven, and causal forests found no subgroup benefiting reproducibly more.

中广东省人民医院临床营养科主任马文君指出，抗炎饮食要调控的是没有明显痛感的慢性低度炎症，而非感冒发烧、伤口红肿类急性炎症。她提出四条原则：食物多样化、优先新鲜天然食材、减少加工食品与快餐、保证足量蔬果；建议植物性食物约占全天三分之二，用全谷杂粮替换部分精白米面，规律摄入豆类与菌菇，并限制红肉、含糖饮料、油炸食品与酒精。

ENMa Wenjun, head of clinical nutrition at Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital, said anti-inflammatory eating targets chronic low-grade inflammation that produces no obvious pain, not the acute inflammation of a fever or a red wound. She set out four principles: eat a variety of foods, favour fresh whole ingredients, cut processed foods and fast food, and ensure ample vegetables and fruit. She suggested plant foods make up about two-thirds of daily intake, swapping some refined grains for whole grains and pseudo-cereals, eating legumes and mushrooms regularly, and limiting red meat, sugary drinks, fried food and alcohol.

中中华医学会糖尿病学分会大会公布《中国糖尿病防治指南（2026版）》要点：章节由20章扩充至27章，首次提出代谢—肝脏—心血管—肾脏（MLCK）综合征概念并建立0—4期分层监测；新增糖化血红蛋白≥7.5%或高于个体化目标1.5%的起始联合治疗路径；体重管理按减重5%、10%、15%分层，并把腰围、腰高比纳入目标；同时新增人工智能、干细胞治疗与肌少症管理。

ENAt the Chinese Diabetes Society congress, highlights of the 2026 China guideline for diabetes prevention and treatment were released. Chapters expanded from 20 to 27, and the guideline introduces the metabolic-liver-cardiovascular-kidney (MLCK) syndrome with a 0 to 4 staging and monitoring framework. A new upfront combination pathway applies when HbA1c is 7.5 percent or higher, or 1.5 percent above an individualised target. Weight goals are tiered at 5, 10 and 15 percent loss and now include waist circumference and waist-to-height ratio, alongside new sections on AI, stem cell therapy and sarcopenia.

ENAt the 2026 national conference on high-quality primary health development, Shanyang county in Shaanxi was named among 18 outstanding innovation cases nationwide, the province's only entry, for its whole-population, whole-lifecycle family doctor contract model. ENXinyi in Guangdong is expanding mutual recognition of medical tests.
