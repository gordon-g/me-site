ENCounterpoint projects global consumer wearables will generate over $1 trillion cumulative revenue in 2026-2032 at a 12% CAGR, with smart rings, pendants and glasses growing fastest. This autumn's launches show the trend: OPPO's FatMax dynamic fat-burning zones, Apple's 5-second heart-rate sampling, Huawei bringing metabolic chronic disease screening to watches and Oura adding nocturnal blood pressure trends — health management is becoming wearables' most durable value anchor.
