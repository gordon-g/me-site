中国内健康科普援引《营养学前沿》逾四千人研究：晚餐钾摄入越多失眠发生率越低，可能与其稳定神经肌肉兴奋性、拮抗钠的作用有关；《欧洲心脏杂志》数据显示钾摄入提升与心血管事件风险下降相关，钠摄入高者每天多1克钾收缩压可显著降低。富含钾的菠菜、豆类、薯类晚餐是低成本干预起点。

ENCiting a Frontiers in Nutrition study of over 4,000 participants, Chinese health communicators note that higher dinner potassium intake is associated with lower insomnia rates, likely via stabilizing neuromuscular excitability and offsetting sodium; European Heart Journal data link higher potassium intake to lower cardiovascular event risk, with each extra gram of potassium notably lowering systolic pressure in high-sodium consumers — potassium-rich greens, beans and tubers at dinner are a low-cost starting point.
