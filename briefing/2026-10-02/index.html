Nature重磅：抗生素治疗期间吃甜食，肠道菌群“伤上加伤”

Nature: eating sweets during antibiotics worsens gut microbiome disruption

中纽约大学朗格尼、纪念斯隆-凯特琳与希望之城团队追踪173名造血干细胞移植患者的9419顿配餐与1000余份粪便样本，发现广谱抗生素治疗期间每多摄入100克甜食，肠道菌群多样性额外下降约21%，粪肠球菌显著扩增；小鼠实验中蔗糖使抗生素诱导的肠球菌扩张放大16-33倍，提示抗生素治疗期间限糖或可保护菌群。

ENTracking 9,419 meals and 1,000-plus stool samples from 173 stem-cell transplant patients, NYU Langone, Memorial Sloan Kettering and City of Hope found that every extra 100 g of sweets during broad-spectrum antibiotic treatment was linked to a further ~21% drop in gut microbial diversity and expansion of Enterococcus; in mice, sucrose amplified antibiotic-induced Enterococcus blooms 16-33-fold, suggesting limiting sugar during antibiotic courses may protect the microbiome.

FM-02

麦吉尔大学Nature：膳食-菌群协同破解癌症免疫治疗“肥胖悖论”

McGill Nature study: diet-microbiome synergy underlies the obesity paradox in immunotherapy

中麦吉尔大学团队用12种模拟人类膳食的小鼠饮食模型发现，抗PD-1疗效取决于“膳食-肠道菌群轴”而非体重或代谢紊乱：致胖饮食中富集的约翰逊乳杆菌与特定膳食协同，经芳香族氨基酸代谢物（如脱氨基酪氨酸）增强T细胞效应；短期饮食切换或补充特定菌株即可逆转免疫治疗耐药，为肿瘤精准营养干预开辟新路径。

ENUsing 12 mouse diet models mimicking human eating patterns, McGill researchers show that anti-PD-1 response depends on the diet-gut axis rather than body weight or metabolic dysfunction: Lactobacillus johnsonii enriched by obesogenic diets acts synergistically with diet via aromatic amino acid metabolites such as desaminotyrosine to boost T-cell effector function; short-term diet switches or specific bacteria reversed immunotherapy resistance, opening new routes for precision nutrition in oncology.

FM-03

中山一院Cell Reports：间歇性禁食经菌群-IPA轴安全抑制血栓

Sun Yat-sen First Hospital in Cell Reports: intermittent fasting curbs thrombosis via gut-microbial IPA

中中山一院常光其、李梓伦团队在Cell Reports报道，隔日禁食通过重塑肠道菌群提升吲哚-3-丙酸（IPA），后者靶向维持血小板Rho GTPase循环稳态，在不增加出血风险的前提下抑制血小板活化；粪菌移植可复制抗血栓效应，动脉血栓患者血浆IPA显著偏低且为独立保护因子，为代谢相关血栓提供新干预靶点。

ENA Cell Reports study from Sun Yat-sen University First Hospital shows alternate-day fasting reshapes the gut microbiota to raise indole-3-propionate (IPA), which keeps platelet Rho GTPase cycling in check and suppresses platelet activation without bleeding risk; fecal microbiota transfer reproduced the effect, and plasma IPA was independently protective in patients with arterial thrombosis, pointing to a new target for metabolic thrombosis.

FM-04

Gut Microbes：抗生素干预人体试验证实IPA几乎完全源于肠道菌群

Gut Microbes: antibiotic intervention confirms IPA is almost entirely gut-microbiota derived

中阿姆斯特丹大学医学中心团队让20名2型糖尿病患者与19名健康人接受7天广谱抗生素干预，结合血浆代谢组与宏基因组证实：血浆吲哚-3-丙酸（IPA）在干预后降至近检出限，几乎完全由菌群产生；IPA水平越低者2型糖尿病风险越高，且与血糖变异性呈负相关，关联菌属集中于毛螺菌科与颤螺菌科，提示菌群整体结构决定代谢保护。

ENAmsterdam UMC gave 20 people with type 2 diabetes and 19 healthy controls a 7-day broad-spectrum antibiotic course: plasma indole-3-propionate (IPA) fell to near detection limits, confirming it is almost entirely microbiota-derived; lower IPA tracked higher T2D risk and greater glycemic variability, with community structure dominated by Lachnospiraceae and Ruminococcaceae — not single producers — determining the metabolic protection.

哈佛Gladyshev团队Nature综述：衰老是细胞身份“语法”的缓慢侵蚀

Nature review from Gladyshev lab: ageing as slow erosion of the grammar of cell identity

中哈佛医学院Gladyshev团队在Nature发表理论综述，提出发育期以高保真方式“写入”细胞身份约束（染色质架构、PRC2介导的抑制），成年后仅靠低保真局部维护，二者不对称导致可预测的表观遗传漂移；约90%年龄相关DNA甲基化增加位于PRC2结合低甲基化区域，可解释跨哺乳动物表观遗传时钟的稳健性，并给出可检验的干预预测。

ENA Nature review from Vadim Gladyshev's Harvard lab argues that cell-identity constraints are written with high fidelity during development but maintained only imperfectly afterwards, and this asymmetry drives predictable epigenetic drift; about 90% of age-related DNA methylation gain sits at PRC2-bound low-methylated regions, explaining the robustness of pan-mammalian epigenetic clocks and yielding testable intervention predictions.

ENIntegrating more than 11,000 transcriptomes from mice, rats, macaques and humans, Tyshkovskiy and Gladyshev built interpretable multi-tissue, multi-species clocks for age and mortality whose accuracy rivals second-generation epigenetic clocks; conserved hallmarks form a modular architecture in which chronic disease mainly accelerates inflammatory modules while caloric restriction and Klotho target metabolic ones, giving a real-time molecular ruler for interventions.

Nature Aging: exercise-derived vesicles carry miR-29 to de-repress Klotho in aged cartilage

中哈佛团队在Nature Aging报道，3个月有氧运动使老年人细胞外囊泡中运动响应性miR-29升高，这些囊泡经表观遗传去抑制长寿基因KL（α-Klotho），恢复软骨细胞年轻表型；过表达miR-29的工程囊泡以KL依赖方式复现效应，关节内注射改善老龄小鼠软骨健康，为运动获益的远端器官递送提供可转化载体。
