中系列报道梳理三地路径：广东实施「四个一万」基层全民健身计划，东莞南城、深圳侨香落地医师、运动处方师与健康管理师组团服务；四川把体卫融合写入新修订的《四川省体育条例》，首批 35 家医疗机构试点，推评估、筛查、测试、方案、跟踪全链条；上海聚焦社区与老年群体。

ENA series reviewing ten years of sport-health integration compares three paths. Guangdong runs a four-ten-thousand primary-level fitness plan, with Dongguan and Shenzhen piloting teams of physicians, exercise prescribers and health managers. Sichuan wrote integration into its revised sports regulation, starting with 35 medical institutions and a full chain from assessment and screening to testing, prescription and follow-up. Shanghai focuses on communities and older adults.
