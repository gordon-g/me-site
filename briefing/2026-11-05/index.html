Kyushu University Turns Muscle Repair Factor Into Super HGF That Resists Age-Related Nitration

中九州大学辰巳隆一团队在《科学报告》报道，三硫化合物 LASSS 与肝细胞生长因子 HGF 按 1:8000 摩尔比混合，可使其与 c-met 受体结合力提升一倍以上并抵抗硝化失活，形成超级 HGF。尾悬吊小鼠预先给予 LASSS 显著减少 HGF 硝化，但未证明能恢复肌力或肌肉量，仍需老年动物实验。

ENRyuichi Tatsumi's group at Kyushu University reported in Scientific Reports that mixing the trisulfide LASSS with hepatocyte growth factor at a 1:8000 molar ratio more than doubled binding to the c-met receptor and protected the protein from nitration-driven inactivation, yielding a so-called Super HGF. In tail-suspended mice, pre-treatment with LASSS cut HGF nitration, though restoration of muscle mass or strength remains unproven and aged-animal studies are still needed.

南京秦虹社区医院体重管理门诊：680 人签约，慢病患者占九成

Nanjing Qinhong Community Weight Clinic Enrols 680 Residents, 91 Percent With Chronic Disease

中南京晨报报道，秦淮区秦虹社区卫生服务中心打造健康体重管理门诊，已有 680 名居民签约，其中慢病患者占 91%，居民体重、内脏脂肪与糖脂指标稳步改善，辖区慢病新发增长放缓。中心以筛查同步宣教、流程简化、上门随访、多网点就近服务提升可及性，下一步推进全域筛查与老年肥胖合并肌少症管理。

ENNanjing Morning Post reports the Qinhong community health centre in Qinhuai district has enrolled 680 residents in its weight management package, 91 percent with chronic disease, with steady gains in weight, visceral fat and glycaemic and lipid markers and slower growth in new chronic cases locally. Screening paired with education, simplified processes, home visits and satellite sites drive uptake; next come district-wide screening and dedicated care for obesity with sarcopenia.
