一个菌群「风险排序谱」：12 种慢病可无创同筛，两项 AUC 超 0.95

One stool sample, twelve diseases: a microbiome index ranks chronic disease risk

中同济大学吴顶峰、尹文靖团队构建 SPECTRA 模型，把 12 个维度菌群指数整合成「风险排序谱」：内部验证平均 AUC 0.92、外部 0.79，血压异常与动脉粥样硬化性心血管病分别达 0.96、0.95；取前三种病时准确率超 96%，对未训练过的病种仍有 0.85。团队已开放 xMICARE 平台，但研究为横断面，尚不能判断因果。

ENWu Dingfeng and Yin Wenjing's team at Tongji University built SPECTRA, a multi-output random-forest model that turns 12 microbiome indices into a ranked risk profile across 12 chronic diseases. Mean AUC reached 0.92 internally and 0.79 in external validation, with 0.96 for abnormal blood pressure and 0.95 for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease; accuracy exceeded 96% when the top three candidates were considered, and held at 0.85 for diseases the model had never seen. The xMICARE platform is open to researchers, but the data are cross-sectional, so causality remains unknown.

饮食失调「从肠计议」：前瞻研究找到暴食发生的菌群风险信号

Eating disorders through the gut: prospective data flag microbiome risk signals for binge eating

中西南大学陈红团队与斯坦福大学 Eric Stice 合作，在《国际进食障碍杂志》发表前瞻研究，把纵向分组分析与鸟枪法宏基因组测序结合。暴食发展者整体菌群多样性无差异，但放线菌门及 11 个属丰度下降，个别弯曲杆菌种升高；清除症状发展者 Roseburia 明显减少。暴食持续者萜类与聚酮类代谢通路更富集，提示菌群可作风险与维持信号。

ENChen Hong's group at Southwest University, working with Eric Stice of Stanford, published a prospective study in the International Journal of Eating Disorders combining longitudinal group analysis with shotgun metagenomic sequencing. In people who went on to develop binge eating, overall diversity did not differ, but Actinobacteria and 11 genera were depleted and certain Campylobacter species rose; those developing purging symptoms showed lower Roseburia. In persistent binge eating, terpenoid and polyketide pathways were enriched — suggesting microbial signals for both risk and maintenance.

千人肠道脂质代谢图谱：小肠菌群的「解脂」基因比结肠更丰富

A thousand-gut lipid map: small-intestine microbes carry richer fat-metabolising gene repertoires

中瓦格宁根大学 Laura C. Valk 团队在《肠道微生物》发表研究，用 88 个隐马尔可夫模型解析逾千份人类宏基因组，系统绘制肠道菌脂质转化基因图谱。小肠菌群脂质代谢基因丰富度显著高于结肠；拟杆菌属肠型 2 富集甾醇磺化与胆碱转化基因，其丰度与血清熊去氧胆酸及磺化衍生物正相关，提示上消化道菌群直接影响宿主心血管代谢。

ENLaura C. Valk's team at Wageningen University published in Gut Microbes a map built from more than 1,000 human metagenomes using 88 hidden Markov models. Lipid-metabolism gene richness was markedly higher in the small intestine than the colon. Bacteroides enterotype 2 was enriched for sterol-sulfation and choline-conversion genes, and that abundance correlated with serum ursodeoxycholic acid and its sulfated derivatives — pointing to upper-gut microbes as direct modulators of host cardiometabolic health.

ENSun Donglei and Xiao Yi's team at Shanghai Jiao Tong University compared three engineered E. coli strains in the ISME Journal and found an ecological window in 8-day-old mice: the commensal strain EcWJ and probiotic strain EcN persisted in crypts, while lab strain MG1655 was cleared quickly after recruiting macrophages and neutrophils; restoring O-antigen synthesis via wbbL extended colonisation. Colonisation strength did not equal efficacy — chassis and payload must match.

生物学年龄每升一档，慢阻肺合并肌少症风险翻倍：CHARLS 4204 人

Higher biological age doubles the odds of sarcopenia in COPD: 4,204-person CHARLS analysis

中山东大学齐鲁医院姜园园团队利用 CHARLS 数据，纳入 4204 名参与者分析生物学年龄与慢阻肺合并肌少症的关联。充分校正后生物学年龄与共病显著正相关（OR 2.52）；限制性立方样条显示关系近似线性，分段回归找到约 60.26 岁的阈值，阈值以下风险随生物学年龄明显升高（OR 1.35），提示加速生物学老化是该共病的关键驱动。

ENJiang Yuanyuan's team at Qilu Hospital of Shandong University used CHARLS data on 4,204 participants to link biological age with sarcopenia in COPD. After full adjustment the association was strong (OR 2.52); restricted cubic splines showed a near-linear relationship, and segmented regression identified a threshold near 60.26 years, below which risk rose clearly with biological age (OR 1.35). Accelerated biological ageing appears to be a key driver of this comorbidity.

ENZhang Zhaoyue of the Liangzhu Laboratory and Jeffrey Friedman's Rockefeller group compared two ketogenic formulas in Cell Metabolism: 5% protein energy sharply cut intake and lowered weight and glucose in C57BL/6J mice, while 10% promoted fat gain and impaired glucose tolerance. The related B6NJ strain showed no such weight loss. The mechanism runs through Nnt deficiency driving hepatic oxidative stress and GDF15, plus Nlrp12-mutant inflammation raising LCN2; NAC partly reversed it.

Health commission answers 'signed but not seen': hospitals told to reserve appointments and beds for family doctors

中针对网民反映家庭医生「签而不约」，国家卫生健康委基层卫生健康司回复称，下一步将推动更多二、三级医院赋予家庭医生一定比例的专家号、住院床位等资源，为其提供门诊、检查、住院服务；进一步落实慢性病长期处方，推动基层用药与二三级医院衔接；加强与签约居民主动联系，为确有需要者提供家庭病床等服务。

ENResponding to residents who say family-doctor contracts are signed but rarely used, the National Health Commission's primary-care department said it will push more secondary and tertiary hospitals to reserve a share of specialist appointments and inpatient beds for family doctors, covering outpatient, testing and admission services. It will also expand long-term prescriptions for chronic disease, align grassroots formularies with hospital formularies, and offer home sickbeds to those who need them.

民盟中央提案：农村慢病防治要按「南北病谱」分区分策

Policy proposal: rural chronic-disease control needs region-specific strategies in China

中民盟中央在《人民日报》提案指出，农村慢病谱地区差异明显：北方以高血压、糖尿病等代谢性疾病高发，南方多见风湿性关节炎、肺结核，统防统治难度大；居民主动体检意识弱、早期诊断率低。建议加大设备与适宜筛查系统投入、把慢病管理接入国家信息系统、改善全科医生考核补偿，并在东部推智慧居家管理、西北西南推远程医疗。

ENIn a People's Daily policy proposal, the China Democratic League notes sharp regional differences in rural chronic disease: metabolic conditions such as hypertension and diabetes dominate in the north, while rheumatoid arthritis and tuberculosis are more common in the south, complicating uniform programmes. It urges investment in equipment and appropriate screening tools, national information-system integration, better pay and evaluation for general practitioners, smart home management in the east and telemedicine in the northwest and southwest.

四川「三医」协同会议：服务从「病人」转向「全民」，强基稳二优三

Sichuan aligns health, insurance and pharma policy, shifting from treating patients to serving everyone

中11 月 19 日，四川省研究「三医」协同工作暨省中医药强省领导小组会议召开。省长施小琳提出推动服务从「以治病为中心」转向「以人民健康为中心」、从服务「病人」转向服务「全民」；深化「人口地图」「健康树」应用，「强基、稳二、优三」优化机构布局，推行医疗集团与医共体，健全长护险评估与支付体系，并通过家庭医生签约推动资源下沉。

ENOn Nov 19 Sichuan held a meeting on aligning medical services, insurance and pharmaceuticals. Governor Shi Xiaolin called for shifting from disease-centred care to people-centred health and from serving patients to serving the whole population. The province will deepen its population-map and health-tree tools, strengthen primary care while stabilising secondary and optimising tertiary institutions, promote medical groups and county consortia, refine long-term care insurance assessment and payment, and use family-doctor contracting to move resources downward.
