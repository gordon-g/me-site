中上海交大医学院团队在《Immunity》揭示，VEGF-B/VEGFR-1 轴经 AKT-MDM2-p53 维持肠道滤泡辅助性 T 细胞干性，保障分泌型 IgA；该轴失灵后 Taurinivorans 菌过度增殖、耗竭肠道牛磺酸，抑制脂肪 AMPK 产热而致肥胖，补充 IgA 或牛磺酸可逆转。

ENA Shanghai Jiao Tong University team reported in Immunity that the VEGF-B/VEGFR-1 axis sustains gut follicular helper T cell stemness through AKT-MDM2-p53, safeguarding secretory IgA. When the axis fails, Taurinivorans overgrows, depletes gut taurine and suppresses adipose AMPK-driven thermogenesis, driving obesity; IgA or taurine supplementation reversed it in mice.

The gut may signal a heart attack before it happens

中光明网援引研究称，急性心梗发作期患者肠道菌群明显失衡——促炎链球菌大幅升高、产丁酸保护菌下降，心梗后半年多可恢复；长期菌群紊乱通过炎症代谢物损伤血管内皮、加速动脉粥样硬化，因此被视作值得关注的预警信号，而非心梗的直接后果。

ENCiting research, Guangming Online reported that gut microbiota shifts markedly during acute myocardial infarction — pro-inflammatory streptococci rise while butyrate-producing protective bacteria fall — and largely normalises within six months. Chronic dysbiosis is seen as an early warning signal that damages endothelium and accelerates atherosclerosis, not a consequence of the event.
