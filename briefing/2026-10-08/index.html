深圳湾实验室×中山大学Nature发文：肠道共生菌产十五烷酸C15:0降胆固醇、缓解动脉粥样硬化

Shenzhen Bay Laboratory and Sun Yat-sen University report in Nature that gut bacterial C15:0 lowers cholesterol and eases atherosclerosis

中新华社深圳10月7日电，深圳湾实验室与中山大学中山医学院合作研究10月7日在线发表于《自然》：肠道共生菌（如脆弱拟杆菌）可自发产生十五烷酸（C15:0），经HMGCR-SREBP2/LDLR通路抑制肝脏胆固醇合成并促进坏胆固醇清除；补充C15:0使动脉粥样硬化小鼠血脂改善、斑块减少，人群样本中血脂异常者该分子水平偏低，为高血脂与心血管代谢防治提供了菌群来源干预的新路径。

ENXinhua reported from Shenzhen that a Shenzhen Bay Laboratory and Sun Yat-sen University team, publishing online in Nature on Oct 7, showed gut commensal bacteria such as Bacteroides uniformis spontaneously produce pentadecanoic acid (C15:0), which suppresses hepatic cholesterol synthesis and promotes LDL clearance via the HMGCR-SREBP2/LDLR axis; C15:0 supplementation improved blood lipids and reduced plaque burden in atherosclerotic mice, and dyslipidemic people showed lower levels, pointing to a microbiome-derived route for cardiometabolic prevention.

麦克马斯特大学Gastroenterology研究：肠道炎症破坏食物代谢菌群，或解释IBD缓解期食物不耐受

McMaster study in Gastroenterology links gut inflammation-altered microbiota to food intolerances in IBD remission

中麦克马斯特大学团队发表于《胃肠病学》的研究显示，肠道炎症会破坏负责降解麸质与乳蛋白的特定菌群，使炎症性肠病患者即便处于缓解期也对新触发食物产生免疫反应；104名缓解期患者中约85%自报食物不耐受，远高于健康对照的33%。结肠炎模型中回补缺失菌株可逆转食物特异性免疫反应，为基于菌群的诊断与治疗指路，也提示个体化营养需考虑菌群对食物成分的加工能力。

ENA McMaster University team reports in Gastroenterology that intestinal inflammation disrupts gut bacteria that break down gluten and dairy proteins, explaining why people with inflammatory bowel disease in remission react to once-tolerated foods; about 85 percent of 104 patients reported food intolerances versus 33 percent of controls, and replenishing depleted bacteria reversed food-specific immune reactions in colitis models, informing microbiome-based diagnosis and personalized nutrition.

中热心肠日报10月7日收录：中国医学科学院放射医学研究所樊赛军、路璐团队发表于International Journal of Biological Sciences的研究显示，口服牛磺酸将受照雄鼠生存率从50%提升至91.7%、雌鼠从66.7%升至100%；雄鼠经乳杆菌-精氨酸/脯氨酸代谢轴促黏膜修复，雌鼠依赖雌激素-AICDA-IgA免疫轴；牛磺酸不干扰放疗杀瘤，支持性别分层的放射防护策略。

直接药物激活AMPK首次证实：酵母、线虫与果蝇寿命延长，部分超25%

Direct pharmacological AMPK activation extends lifespan in yeast, worms and flies, by over 25 percent in some settings

中10月7日报道，MRC医学科学实验室、帝国理工学院与科隆大学团队在Aging Cell发表研究：用化合物991直接激活细胞能量传感器AMPK，使裂殖酵母、线虫与果蝇寿命延长、部分实验超25%，且在AMPK功能缺失时效应消失，首次证明直接靶向AMPK可带来长寿获益；处理三周的小鼠肝脏出现促长寿蛋白组模式并抑制mTOR通路。作者提醒过度激活反而缩短寿命，距离人类抗衰应用仍很遥远。

ENReported on Oct 7: a team from the MRC Laboratory of Medical Sciences, Imperial College London and the University of Cologne showed in Aging Cell that directly activating the cellular energy sensor AMPK with compound 991 extended lifespan in fission yeast, nematode worms and fruit flies, by more than 25 percent in some experiments, with the effect vanishing when AMPK was disabled; three-week treatment of mice induced a pro-longevity proteomic profile with mTOR suppression. The authors caution that excess activation shortened life and human translation remains distant.

Zombie cells drive aging — scientists now aim to rejuvenate the immune cleanup crew rather than just kill the cells

中Live Science 10月7日发表深度报道：衰老细胞（俗称僵尸细胞）随年龄在组织中累积并释放促炎因子，而负责清除它们的巨噬细胞功能同步衰退。7月发表于《科学》的小鼠研究发现，老年巨噬细胞EP2受体增多、吞噬衰老细胞能力下降，敲除EP2或阻断其信号可恢复清除功能，改善认知、肌力、心功能与全身炎症；研究者认为焕新免疫系统或比直接杀伤衰老细胞更安全，有望成为senolytics之外的新路径。

ENA Live Science feature published on Oct 7 explains how senescent zombie cells accumulate with age while the macrophages that clear them slow down; a July Science study in mice found aged macrophages overexpress the EP2 receptor, impairing engulfment of senescent cells, and blocking EP2 restored clearance and improved cognition, muscle strength, cardiac function and systemic inflammation, suggesting immune rejuvenation may offer a safer complement to senolytics.

中科学网10月7日报道，广州医科大学边英杰课题组10月6日发表于《细胞》的研究揭示肿瘤与T细胞之间的维生素B2竞争：肿瘤优先经SLC52A2掠夺VB2，令依赖SLC52A3摄取VB2的T细胞陷入缺乏，线粒体呼吸与谷胱甘肽再生受损、触发铁死亡并削弱抗肿瘤免疫；敲除肿瘤SLC52A2或补充VB2可增强CAR-T疗效，肿瘤SLC52A2高表达与患者生存期差相关，而膳食VB2摄入与癌症风险降低相关。
