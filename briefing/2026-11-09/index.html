中复旦大学附属中山医院尹豪豪、徐辉雄团队在《先进科学》报告，MASH 患者肠道中邓肯氏栖粪杆菌显著减少。团队用右手性金纳米酶包被该菌构建 F@D-Au，八周干预后小鼠肝脏脂肪变性、炎症与纤维化明显逆转，血脑屏障恢复、海马炎症减轻、空间记忆改善，粪便犬尿酸与血清亚油酸同步回升。

A gut microbial metabolite of methotrexate is no waste: DAMPA eases colitis via the folate receptor

中威斯康星大学团队在《细胞·宿主与微生物》发现，肠菌羧肽酶 G2 把甲氨蝶呤转化为 DAMPA。炎症性肠病患者与结肠炎小鼠该酶及 DAMPA 显著减少，盲肠 DAMPA 降约八成。DAMPA 以 2.07 纳摩尔结合叶酸受体α，激活 STAT3、促线粒体自噬；口服五十天粪便脂质运载蛋白-2 降六成。

ENA University of Wisconsin-Madison team reports in Cell Host and Microbe that bacterial carboxypeptidase G2 converts methotrexate into DAMPA. Both the enzyme and DAMPA fall sharply in IBD patients and colitic mice, with cecal DAMPA down about 80 percent. DAMPA binds folate receptor alpha at 2.07 nanomolar, activates STAT3 and drives mitophagy; 50 days of oral dosing cut fecal lipocalin-2 by roughly 60 percent.

ENIn PNAS, MD Anderson researchers show that a 24-hour fast expands Akkermansia muciniphila in the small intestine. 河南把「健康优先」写进党代会报告：预防为主、均衡普惠、优质高效三条线

Henan writes health-in-all-policies into its party congress report

中河南省第十二次党代会报告把「实施健康优先发展战略」列为健康河南建设的重要任务，提出把健康融入所有政策。解读给出三条线：预防为主，常态化开展健康体重管理与三高共管、多病同防；均衡普惠，推进县域医共体与家庭医生签约；优质高效，依托 12 个国家区域医疗中心与三级专科联动网络理顺分级诊疗。

ENThe report to Henan's 12th party congress makes a health-first development strategy a core task for building a healthy province, embedding health in all policies. Commentary highlights three tracks: prevention, with routine weight management and joint control of the three highs; equitable access, through county medical communities and family doctor contracts; and quality, anchored by 12 national regional medical centers and tiered specialty networks.
