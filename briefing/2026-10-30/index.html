《Microbiome》：压力先引爆肠道活性氧，短链脂肪酸先崩、菌群结构后变

Microbiome: Stress first triggers an intestinal ROS burst, collapsing short-chain fatty acids before the microbiome shifts

中斑马鱼实验中，应激数小时内肠道远端活性氧骤升，此时菌群结构未变，但厌氧发酵被抑制、短链脂肪酸急降；上皮产能受损后肠腔低氧屏障失守，兼性致病菌得以扩张。拘束应激小鼠重现同一顺序，清除 ROS 或恢复上皮产能即可阻断失调。

ENIn zebrafish, intestinal reactive oxygen species surged in the distal gut within hours of stress, before any change in microbial composition. Anaerobic fermentation was selectively suppressed and short-chain fatty acid output fell sharply; epithelial PPARγ-dependent metabolism then weakened, the hypoxic barrier eroded and facultative pathobionts expanded. Restraint-stressed mice showed the same sequence. Neutralizing intestinal ROS or restoring epithelial bioenergetics prevented dysbiosis despite ongoing stress, pointing to the hypoxic barrier as a target.

Nature: Four days of the wrong diet at disease onset leaves inflammatory memory for months

中银屑病样皮肤炎症模型显示，高脂饮食只需在发病前 3 天至发病后 1 天这 4 天窗口给予，即可放大炎症，与是否肥胖无关；已肥胖小鼠在该窗口换低脂饮食同样能抵消。该窗口塑造组织驻留记忆 CD4 T 细胞，使 25 天后再次激发复发更重，效应持续至少 6.5 个月。

ENUsing a psoriasis-like skin inflammation model, researchers found that a high-fat diet given only during a four-day window — three days before to one day after onset — amplified inflammation, independent of obesity. 《Aging Cell》：星形胶质细胞 FGF13 上调，让小鼠全身衰老减速

Aging Cell: Boosting astrocytic FGF13 slows whole-body aging in mice

中FGF13 随年龄在星形胶质细胞中下降，与自噬蛋白 Phafin2 结合促进自噬。敲除老年小鼠星形胶质细胞 FGF13 会加重衰老、提高循环炎症因子；反向过表达后，水迷宫表现接近年轻鼠，血中 IFN-γ、IL-6 明显下降，皮肤、肝、脾、肾衰老标志减少，年龄相关增重减轻。

ENFGF13 declines with age in astrocytes and binds Phafin2 to promote autophagy. Guangdong launches enterprise health service platform as 'Yue Xiaoyi' takes over post-checkup care

中10 月 29 日，2026 健康产业创新发展大会在广州举行，广东省企联医疗健康服务平台启动，首期项目「粤小医」由微医数智医院运营，构建「个性化体检—风险评估—干预方案—跟踪随访」闭环；以医疗大模型与健康智能体联动智能指环、动态血糖监测，生成数字孪生健康画像并由专属团队持续管护。

ENAt the 2026 Health Industry Innovation Conference in Guangzhou on October 29, the Guangdong enterprise federation's medical and health service platform launched with Yue Xiaoyi as its first project. Operated technically by WeDoctor's digital hospital, it builds a proactive loop from personalised checkups through risk assessment and intervention to follow-up, covering health records, screening, chronic disease management and care coordination. A medical large model and health agents link smart rings and continuous glucose monitors into a digital-twin health profile, with health managers, family doctors and tertiary specialists following up.
