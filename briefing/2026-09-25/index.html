ENNorwegian molecular diagnostics company Genetic Analysis announced that its Canadian patent application has been allowed, protecting an in vitro diagnostic method that analyzes gut microbiota composition to identify bacterial patterns predicting which irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) patients are more likely to respond to fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT). 中美国德州大学 MD 安德森癌症中心团队在 Cell Reports Medicine 发表粪菌移植（FMT）一线治疗免疫检查点抑制剂相关腹泻和结肠炎（IMDC）的临床数据：13 例患者中 10 例（76.9%）达到临床响应，临床改善中位时间仅 1.5 天；应答者 FMT 后肠道微生物多样性增加，浆细胞、多种 T 细胞亚群等免疫细胞丰度下降。研究提示 FMT 有望从难治性结肠炎的补救方案前移为一线干预选项。

ENResearchers at the University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center published clinical data in Cell Reports Medicine on first-line fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) for immune checkpoint inhibitor-mediated diarrhea and colitis (IMDC): 10 of 13 patients (76.9%) achieved clinical response, with a median time to improvement of just 1.5 days. Responders showed increased gut microbial diversity after FMT and reductions in plasma cells and multiple T-cell subsets.
