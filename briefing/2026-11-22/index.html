中11月20日聊城市卫健委发布会披露，该市全民健康信息平台按「市级统建、县级共享、基层使用」连接2300余套终端、打通16类信息板块，电子病历、健康档案等四大数据库实时归集；基层引进清华DUCG人工智能辅助诊断，覆盖1700余种疾病。全市93家机构实现省域内检查检验结果互认，影像357项、检验151项；39家二级以上医院推行「一次挂号、多日有效」。

ENAt a Nov 20 briefing, Liaocheng's health commission said its city-built health information platform links more than 2,300 terminals across 16 data domains, with real-time aggregation of electronic records, health files and other core databases. Primary care has adopted Tsinghua's DUCG AI diagnostic support covering over 1,700 diseases. Ninety-three institutions now recognize each other's results within the province, covering 357 imaging and 151 lab items, and 39 hospitals offer multi-day validity for a single registration.

Guangzhou's Panyu District Fields 217 Family Doctor Teams as a Three-Professional Weight Clinic Sets an Example

中广州番禺以25间健康驿站、23间社区卫生服务机构和217个家庭医生团队织密基层网络，特色科室覆盖中医治未病、康复、体重管理、呼吸慢病、内分泌与老年医学。钟村街社区卫生服务中心打造「三师共管」医防融合体重管理门诊，以个性化营养处方与中医适宜技术为核心。2025年度辖区社区门诊量246.49万人次，较2022年增长54%。

ENPanyu district in Guangzhou has built a dense primary-care network of 25 health stations, 23 community health institutions and 217 family doctor teams, with specialty clinics spanning preventive Chinese medicine, rehabilitation, weight management, respiratory chronic disease, endocrinology and geriatrics. Zhongcun Street's center runs a three-professional, prevention-treatment integrated weight clinic built on personalized nutrition prescriptions and appropriate TCM techniques. Community outpatient visits reached 2.46 million in 2025, up 54% from 2022.

湖北老年医学基层适宜人才咸宁培训班开班，八位省市专家授课

Hubei Trains Grassroots Geriatric Talent in Xianning With Eight Provincial and Municipal Experts

中11月20日至21日，咸宁市卫健委主办、咸宁市中医医院承办的2026年湖北省老年医学基层适宜人才（咸宁）培训班举行，来自乡镇卫生院、社区卫生服务中心及医养结合机构的老年病相关医师参加。课程聚焦高血压、糖尿病等常见慢病规范化管理，以及老年焦虑抑郁的早期识别与干预，并涵盖老年护理需求评估、合理用药、康复护理、居家医疗与中医药适宜技术。

ENOn Nov 20-21 the Xianning health commission and the city's TCM hospital ran the 2026 Hubei training course for grassroots geriatric talent, drawing physicians from township hospitals, community health centers and integrated elder-care facilities. Sessions focused on standardized management of hypertension and diabetes and early recognition of anxiety and depression in older adults, plus geriatric nursing assessment, rational prescribing, rehabilitation, home care and appropriate TCM techniques.
