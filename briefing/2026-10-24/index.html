中耶鲁大学Vishwa Deep Dixit团队在Cell发表研究：一年期热量限制显著提升健康中年人内脏脂肪中牛磺酸水平，且与炎症负相关。机制上，牛磺酸经NLRP3危险信号诱导外流是上游事件，通过调控巨噬细胞NLRP3炎性小体活化减轻炎症；老年小鼠补充牛磺酸4个月可降低炎症因子、改善健康，为干预炎症衰老提供新靶点。

ENYale's Vishwa Deep Dixit lab reports in Cell that one year of caloric restriction raised taurine levels in visceral fat of healthy middle-aged adults, inversely correlated with inflammation. Mechanistically, NLRP3 danger-signal-induced taurine efflux is an upstream event; taurine modulates NLRP3 inflammasome activation in macrophages. Four months of taurine supplementation lowered inflammatory cytokines and improved health in aged mice, offering a new target against inflammaging.
