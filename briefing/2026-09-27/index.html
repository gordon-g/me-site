中国务院食安办联合中央文明办、农业农村部、市场监管总局等 27 个部门和单位于 9 月 22 日在京启动 2026 年全国食品安全宣传周，主题为「尚德守法 共享食安——深化全链条监管」。宣传周期间将举办 40 余场部委主题日活动，涵盖法规标准宣贯、典型案例发布与食品安全营养科普，并通过「你点我检」等公众参与形式推进社会共治。

ENChina's State Council food safety office launched the 2026 National Food Safety Awareness Week in Beijing on Sep 22 with 27 departments under the theme of deepening whole-chain regulation. More than 40 ministerial theme-day events will cover standards training, case releases and nutrition education, with public participation mechanisms such as the 'you order, we test' program.

全国儿童青少年「五健」促进行动工作交流会暨培训班召开

National Training Session Held for the Children and Adolescents 'Five Healths' Promotion Initiative

中全国儿童青少年「五健」促进行动工作交流会暨培训班近日召开，围绕视力、体重、心理、骨骼、口腔五大健康促进行动部署落地路径，推动各地整合学校、医疗机构与家庭场景开展筛查、干预与健康教育。该行动与「十五五」健康规划中儿童青少年健康促进行动相衔接，是肥胖防控与近视、脊柱问题协同治理的重要抓手。

ENA national exchange and training session for the 'Five Healths' initiative for children and adolescents laid out implementation pathways across vision, weight, mental health, bone and oral health, mobilizing schools, medical institutions and families for screening, intervention and education. The program aligns with the 15th Five-Year health plan's child and adolescent health promotion actions.
