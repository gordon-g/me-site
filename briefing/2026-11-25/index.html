南医大团队揭示迷走神经刺激抗癫痫的肠-脑轴机制

Chinese team uncovers the gut-brain mechanism behind vagus nerve stimulation in epilepsy

中珠江医院郭燕舞、孙海涛团队在 Cell Host & Microbe 发表研究，纵向采集患者术前及术后 6、12 个月粪便与血浆：术后产短链脂肪酸菌群富集，粪便丁酸下降、血浆丁酸升高，降幅与发作缓解正相关。机制为结肠 MCT1 转运丁酸、结状神经节 GPR41 经迷走神经上行激活孤束核，敲低 GPR41 或离断迷走神经均减弱保护效应。

ENA team at Zhujiang Hospital tracked stool and plasma samples from epilepsy patients before and at 6 and 12 months after vagus nerve stimulation (VNS), publishing in Cell Host & Microbe. After VNS, short-chain fatty acid producers including Akkermansia were enriched, fecal butyrate fell while plasma butyrate rose, and the size of the fecal drop correlated with seizure relief. Mechanistically, colonic transporter MCT1 moves butyrate to the nodose ganglion, where receptor GPR41 signals up the vagus to the nucleus tractus solitarius; knocking down GPR41 or cutting the vagus abolished the protection.

中绍兴大学钱海丰团队在 Cell Host & Microbe 分析 12,238 份人类肠道宏基因组，筛出 159 种与耐药相关致病类群稳定负相关的共生菌，中位检出率 45.9%，多属毛螺菌科与瘤胃球菌科。对普氏栖粪杆菌 2,053 个基因组做深度学习挖掘，得 6,737 条候选抗菌肽；小鼠补充 15 天后耐药基因总丰度显著下降，菌群网络更稳健。

ENQian Haifeng's group at Shaoxing University analysed 12,238 human gut metagenomes and reported in Cell Host & Microbe 159 commensal species consistently negatively associated with antibiotic-resistance gene (ARG)-carrying pathogens. Median detection rate was 45.9 percent, with enrichment in Lachnospiraceae and Ruminococcaceae. Deep-learning mining of 2,053 Faecalibacterium prausnitzii genomes yielded 6,737 candidate antimicrobial peptides; in antibiotic-disturbed mice, 15 days of supplementation lowered total ARG abundance and improved network stability.

Nature Communications：驱动 IBD 菌群失调的是炎症本身，而非疾病标签

In IBD, inflammation itself — not the disease label — drives dysbiosis

中研究用宏基因组中残留人类 DNA 的比例构建客观「炎症指数」，与粪便钙卫蛋白高度相关。炎症越重，菌群与健康人差异越大，该规律在健康对照中同样成立；匹配炎症水平后，IBD 患者与健康人的菌群差异显著缩小。菌株层面还发现，丰度上与 IBD 强相关的物种内部菌株分化反而不明显，患者肠道菌株的持久性显著降低。

ENResearchers built an objective inflammation index from the share of human DNA left in metagenomic data, which correlated closely with fecal calprotectin. The more inflamed the gut, the larger the divergence from healthy microbiota — a pattern that also held in healthy controls; after matching inflammation levels, differences between IBD patients and controls shrank markedly. At strain level, species strongly linked to IBD showed less internal strain differentiation, and bacterial persistence in patients was significantly lower.

ENInes Thiele's group at the University of Galway followed one patient with colonic Crohn's disease across 51 time points over 3.6 years, building a personalised host-microbiome metabolic model published in Gut Microbes.
