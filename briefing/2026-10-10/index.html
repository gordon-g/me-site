Science：为什么打疫苗容易发烧？肠道菌群早已“ preset ”免疫基调

Science: Gut microbes help set the body's fever response to vaccination

中马克斯·普朗克研究所Ruth Ley团队整合171名成人纵向体温监测与多组学发现：肠道菌群通过持续高产鞭毛蛋白预致敏先天免疫，奠定疫苗接种后发热反应基调；高发热者富集毛螺菌科Waltera属并伴低度肠道炎症，西式饮食与羧甲基纤维素促进鞭毛蛋白产生，而植物性饮食者发热反应更弱。

ENA Max Planck team led by Ruth Ley combined longitudinal temperature monitoring and multi-omics in 171 adults, showing that gut microbes constantly producing flagellin pre-sensitize innate immunity and set the tone of post-vaccination fever. High-responders carried more Waltera (Lachnospiraceae) with low-grade gut inflammation; Western diet and CMC boosted flagellin, while plant-based diets were linked to weaker fever responses.

GUT: Genes set susceptibility, the gut drives disease — new ideas for fatty liver intervention

中GUT发表研究揭示基因缺陷与西方饮食“双打击”下肠屏障失守，菌群代谢的长链神经酰胺与次级胆汁酸飙升，压制肝细胞线粒体功能并驱动脂肪肝。粪菌移植健康菌群或口服巴氏灭活Akk菌及其膜蛋白Amuc1100均可修复屏障、降低有害神经酰胺，即便在高脂饮食下依然有效。

ENA GUT study shows that a 'double hit' of genetic defects plus Western diet breaks down the gut barrier, letting microbial long-chain ceramides and secondary bile acids impair hepatocyte mitochondria and drive fatty liver. Fecal microbiota transfer or pasteurized A. muciniphila and its membrane protein Amuc1100 restored the barrier and lowered harmful ceramides even under high-fat feeding.

中研究用条形码追踪拟杆菌发现：肠道共生菌在小鼠体内数周内即可快速适应性进化，复杂多糖环境更能重现体内选择压力，且近缘谱系可演化出盲肠或粪便偏好等可遗传空间生态位，却不影响跨宿主传播能力。这一动态演化图景为设计更稳定定植的益生菌疗法提供理论锚点。

LM-01

Science：894只犬表观遗传时钟解释“大型犬为何短寿”，转座子成关键线索

Science: Epigenetic clocks in 894 dogs explain why big dogs die young, pointing to transposable elements

中亚利桑那州立大学团队基于犬类衰老项目894只犬的1640个甲基化组构建表观遗传时钟：时钟可预测死亡风险，且表观衰老在生命早期最快；体型更大、寿命更短的公犬分子衰老加速，性别相关变化集中于X染色体，体型相关变化聚焦转座子区域，为“衰老速度决定寿命”提供了跨物种自然实验证据。

ENUsing 1,640 methylomes from 894 dogs in the Dog Aging Project, ASU researchers built an epigenetic clock that predicts mortality, with epigenetic aging fastest early in life. Larger, shorter-lived male dogs showed accelerated molecular aging; sex effects concentrated on the X chromosome while size effects were pronounced at transposable elements — a natural cross-species experiment linking aging pace to lifespan.

LM-03

每天酸奶+饮食管理+运动：日本小型研究显示男性生物衰老速度放缓约2.2%

Daily yogurt, diet counseling and exercise slowed biological aging by about 2.2% in Japanese men

中日本一项针对48名50-74岁超重男性的研究显示：每日100克无糖酸奶配合减少过食与含糖饮料的饮食指导、每周至少三次30分钟步行或踏步运动，持续干预后DunedinPACE测算的生物衰老速度放缓约2.2%，效果与25%热量限制研究相当，且独立于体重下降，提示简单生活方式组合即可影响衰老速率。

AJCN随机试验：抗阻训练下素食者与杂食者增肌接近，杂食者肌肉量多增约70%

AJCN RCT: Vegetarians and omnivores gain strength alike on resistance training, but omnivores gain ~70% more muscle mass

中阿姆斯特丹应用科学大学团队让108名55岁以上成人随机采用素食或杂食饮食并进行每周三次抗阻训练、蛋白质≥1.2克/公斤：12周后两组肌力与体功能改善相当，但杂食组四肢肌肉量增幅约0.66公斤，比素食组（0.38公斤）高约70%；素食组中蛋白摄入越高增肌越多，提示植物基饮食者更需关注蛋白量与质。

ENIn a randomized trial from Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, 108 adults aged 55+ followed vegetarian or omnivorous diets with resistance training three times weekly and at least 1.2 g protein/kg. After 12 weeks, strength and function improved similarly, but omnivores gained ~70% more limb muscle (0.66 vs 0.38 kg). Vegetarians gained more muscle with higher protein intake, underscoring protein quantity and quality for plant-based eaters.

中NutraIngredients报道一项随机交叉研究：单次咖啡因摄入可短暂升高动脉僵硬度和血压，而提前补充富含硝酸盐的甜菜根汁可显著减弱这一反应，机制可能与硝酸盐-亚硝酸盐-一氧化氮通路改善血管内皮功能有关。研究为咖啡爱好者提供了膳食硝酸盐这一低成本血管保护思路，仍需更大规模试验确认。

ENA randomized crossover study reported by NutraIngredients found that a single dose of caffeine transiently raises arterial stiffness and blood pressure, but pre-loading with nitrate-rich beetroot juice significantly blunted this response, likely via the nitrate-nitrite-NO pathway improving endothelial function. Dietary nitrate offers a low-cost vascular strategy for coffee drinkers, pending larger trials.
