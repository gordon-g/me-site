《自然·代谢》：首个临床研究显示限时进食可改善早期亨廷顿病相关指标

Nature Metabolism: First clinical study links time-restricted eating to improved markers in early Huntington's disease

中俄勒冈健康与科学大学团队在《自然·代谢》发表首个针对亨廷顿病的限时进食临床研究：20名早期患者将每日进食限制在6—8小时窗口并坚持12周，体重与瘦体重保持稳定，综合统一亨廷顿病评定量表平均改善0.5分（该评分通常每年下降约1分），神经丝轻链平均下降13%，血细胞线粒体活性多项指标改善。团队正筹备更大规模随机对照试验。

ENAn OHSU-led team published the first clinical study of time-restricted eating in Huntington's disease in Nature Metabolism: 20 early-stage patients limited eating to a 6–8 hour daily window for 12 weeks. They maintained weight and lean mass, improved 0.5 points on the composite UHDRS (which typically declines ~1 point per year), saw neurofilament light fall 13%, and showed improved mitochondrial activity in blood cells. A larger randomized trial is being planned.

China releases 2026 guideline for autism intervention and rehabilitation in children: best window is before age six

中国家卫生健康委9月22日公布《孤独症谱系障碍儿童干预康复指南（2026年版）》，明确目前针对孤独症病因和发病机制的特异性治疗方法仍有限，主要干预方法为康复训练，最佳治疗期为6岁前、越早干预效果越好。指南围绕早期筛查、家庭参与、康复机构规范与转介路径给出系统要求，为基层开展儿童心理行为健康服务提供依据。

ENChina's National Health Commission published the 2026 edition of its intervention and rehabilitation guideline for children with autism spectrum disorder, noting that disease-specific treatments remain limited, rehabilitation training is the mainstay, and the best window is before age six — the earlier the better. The guideline systematizes early screening, family participation, provider standards and referral pathways for grassroots child mental and behavioral health services.

AI 重塑健康信息生态，营养品牌面临“信任测试”

Industry faces trust test as AI reshapes health information

中NutraIngredients 9月23日刊文称，AI正在重塑消费者获取健康与营养信息的方式，营养品牌面临一场“信任测试”：生成式工具放大了伪科学与夸大宣称的传播风险，也带来个性化沟通的新机会。受访行业人士建议，品牌应主动投资循证内容、透明溯源与第三方验证，把“科学可信”作为AI信息生态中的核心竞争资产。

ENAI is reshaping how consumers obtain health and nutrition information, and nutrition brands face a trust test: generative tools amplify the spread of pseudoscience and exaggerated claims while creating new opportunities for personalized communication. Industry voices recommend investing in evidence-based content, transparent sourcing and third-party validation, making scientific credibility the core competitive asset in the AI information ecosystem.
