中阿姆斯特丹大学团队在《Nature Aging》分析小鼠 10 种组织及线虫、果蝇与人类样本，发现脂质酰基链随年龄延长、短中链耗竭，是跨物种衰老标志；雷帕霉素与短期运动只改变部分脂质，饮食限制可在年轻与老年小鼠心脏全面逆转。肌肉 Plb1 表达与寿命负相关，敲低线虫同源基因可延寿。

ENAnalysing ten mouse tissues plus nematode, fruit-fly and human samples, an Amsterdam UMC team reports in Nature Aging that lipid acyl chains lengthen and short- and medium-chain species are depleted with age — a conserved aging signature. Rapamycin and short-term exercise altered only subsets of lipids, while dietary restriction broadly reversed the shift in young and old mouse hearts. Muscle Plb1 expression correlated negatively with lifespan, and knocking down its worm orthologue extended life.

中海南白沙建成全省首个县域医共体智能共享中药房，11 个乡镇 13 家卫生院统一审方、煎煮、配送；同步建成县乡慢病管理中心。临高梳理 57 种慢特病，25107 名高血压、13654 名糖尿病患者可在乡镇完成认定、开方与结算，并摸排出 676 人的送药需求，建立三级药品联购覆盖 169 个村卫生室。

ENBaisha county built Hainan's first county-wide smart shared pharmacy for traditional medicine, with 13 township hospitals across 11 townships handling unified prescription review, decoction and delivery so mountain residents wait at home for their medicine, alongside county-township chronic disease centres and integrated clinics. Lingao mapped 57 chronic special-disease categories, letting 25,107 hypertension and 13,654 diabetes patients be certified, prescribed and reimbursed at township level; screening 2,646 reimbursement records identified 676 people needing home delivery, supported by three-tier purchasing across 169 village clinics.

2026 功能医学行业调研：场景分散、无绝对龙头，瓶颈在标准、人才与支付

2026 functional medicine survey: fragmented settings, no dominant player, with standards, talent and payment as bottlenecks

中中研普华《2026—2030 年功能医学产业报告》指出，行业仍处早期向成长期过渡：服务经健康管理机构、体检中心、民营门诊、体重与营养中心落地，检测覆盖营养元素、肠道菌群、食物不耐受与氧化应激；瓶颈是标准化不足、人才稀缺、支付有限与效果验证不完善。报告判断主力市场为自费加商保。

ENA Zhongyan Puhua report on China's functional medicine industry for 2026–2030 finds the sector still transitioning from early to growth stage: services are delivered through health management firms, check-up centres, private clinics and weight and nutrition centres, with testing spanning nutrients, gut microbiota, food intolerance, hormones and oxidative stress. Bottlenecks are weak standardisation, scarce trained staff, limited payment mechanisms and immature outcome validation. The report sees self-pay plus commercial insurance as the main market, with AI interpretation and GLP-1 companion management as growth areas.
