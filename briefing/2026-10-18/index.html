ENAhead of the Double Ninth Festival, Abbott ran its fourth consecutive charity campaign with the Shanghai Charity Foundation, donating millions of yuan of medical nutrition products and supporting geriatric screenings and education across more than 20 provinces. Clinical nutrition experts underscored quality protein for muscle health in older adults and guided use of medical nutrition under professional supervision.

China CDC-Backed Handbook: “Three Highs Plus Shingles” Tips and a 16-Character Mantra for Safe Autumn Outings

中针对重阳出行高峰，中国疾控中心指导下发布的秋季出行健康手册提出“药带齐，苗早问；慢病不停药，行程不硬扛”十六字口诀。武汉协和医院专家提醒：糖尿病患者带状疱疹风险最高可高出60%，心血管患者高出39%；出行前应做好健康评估、规律饮食、带齐监测设备，带状疱疹出疹后72小时内启动抗病毒治疗效果最佳。

ENFor the Double Ninth travel peak, a handbook issued under China CDC guidance distills autumn travel tips into a 16-character mantra covering medications, vaccines and steady treatment. Wuhan Union Hospital experts note diabetes raises shingles risk by up to 60% and cardiovascular disease by 39%; travelers should assess their health beforehand, and antiviral therapy works best within 72 hours of rash onset.
