为何肠炎易致食物不耐受？菌群“降解防线”崩溃是关键，益生菌或可修复

Why gut inflammation breeds food intolerance: collapsed microbial degradation is key, and probiotics may repair it

中麦克马斯特大学团队发表于Gastroenterology的研究显示：肠道炎症会耗竭梭菌、双歧杆菌等降解食物抗原的关键菌群，使完整乳蛋白、麸质入血，触发肥大细胞与IgE介导的食物不耐受；炎症性肠病患者自报食物不耐受率超85%。小鼠实验中补充产孢梭菌或长双歧杆菌可恢复抗原降解能力、显著减轻致敏反应，提示未来或可用“修复菌群代谢功能”替代单纯饮食回避。

ENA McMaster University team reports in Gastroenterology that gut inflammation depletes Clostridium and Bifidobacterium species that degrade food antigens, letting intact milk proteins and gluten enter circulation and triggering mast-cell and IgE-mediated food intolerance; over 85% of IBD patients self-report food intolerance. In mice, supplementing C. sporogenes or B. longum restored antigen degradation and markedly reduced sensitization, hinting that repairing microbial metabolic function may replace strict dietary avoidance.

Peking University team: gut microbial bile acid dysregulation damages the blood-brain barrier in Parkinson's; L. reuteri may help

ENA team led by Rongsheng Zhao and Junliang Yuan reports in Cell Reports Medicine that taurochenodeoxycholic acid (TCDCA) is elevated in Parkinson's patients and correlates with severity, driven by reduced gut microbial bile salt hydrolase (bsh) abundance. TCDCA promotes cerebral vascular smooth muscle apoptosis via TGR5-PKCα signaling and damages the blood-brain barrier. Supplementing BSH-rich Lactobacillus reuteri lowered TCDCA and eased parkinsonian symptoms in mice, pointing to a new gut-brain axis target.

Nature Aging: lipid elongation emerges as a conserved, druggable hallmark of aging across species

中阿姆斯特丹大学团队10月6日发表于Nature Aging的研究提出“脂质延长”这一新衰老标志：脂质酰基链随衰老跨物种（小鼠、线虫、果蝇、人类）系统性变长，人类心脏病进展中也可见类似重塑，饮食限制则可缩短小鼠心肌脂质链。在线虫中敲低脂质重塑因子Plb1可逆转脂质延长并以长度依赖方式延长寿命，重庆大学庞珊珊同期评论称其为衰老的因果因素。

ENA University of Amsterdam team reports in Nature Aging (Oct 6) a new hallmark of aging: lipid acyl chains systematically elongate across species (mice, worms, flies and humans), a remodeling also seen in human heart disease, while dietary restriction shortens lipid chains in mouse hearts. Knocking down the lipid remodeling factor Plb1 in C. elegans reversed elongation and extended lifespan in a length-dependent manner; a companion commentary by Shanshan Pang calls lipid length a causal factor in aging.

为何“僵尸细胞”随衰老堆积？Nature Aging：自噬 declined 让巨噬细胞无力清除

Why 'zombie cells' pile up with age: Nature Aging study shows declining autophagy leaves macrophages unable to clear them

中爱因斯坦医学院Cuervo团队10月5日发表于Nature Aging的研究揭示衰老细胞堆积新机制：年龄相关的伴侣介导自噬（CMA）下降既改变衰老细胞分泌谱，又削弱巨噬细胞吞噬功能，形成恶性循环。老年小鼠中用激活剂CA77.1恢复CMA五个月，多器官衰老细胞负荷、炎症与纤维化减少，肺纤维化模型病情减轻，提示或可“修复免疫清除功能”而非直接杀伤衰老细胞。

ENAna Maria Cuervo's team at Albert Einstein College of Medicine reports in Nature Aging (Oct 5) a new mechanism behind senescent cell accumulation: age-related decline in chaperone-mediated autophagy (CMA) both alters senescent cells' secretome and impairs macrophage phagocytosis, creating a vicious cycle. In aged mice, five months of CMA activation with CA77.1 reduced senescent cell burden, inflammation and fibrosis across organs and eased pulmonary fibrosis, suggesting restoring immune clearance rather than killing senescent cells directly.

种子大小的无线神经刺激器问世：无需手术即可调控神经、缓解慢性疼痛

A seed-sized wireless nerve stimulator could tame chronic pain without surgery

中科学家开发出一种可注射的种子大小神经调控装置，无需手术、电池或导线，由体外无线供能，可精确调控目标神经活动，在早期测试中对慢性疼痛与运动障碍展现出潜力。该技术若通过后续临床验证，有望为药物疗效不佳的慢性疼痛患者提供微创选择，也为疼痛管理、康复医学与神经接口研究提供新工具，是“电子药物”方向的又一进展。

ENScientists have developed an injectable, seed-sized nerve-modulation device that works without surgery, batteries or wires, powered wirelessly from outside the body to precisely control target nerve activity, showing promise for chronic pain and movement disorders in early testing. If later clinical trials validate it, the technology could offer a minimally invasive option for drug-refractory chronic pain and a new tool for rehabilitation medicine and neural interface research — another step for 'electroceuticals'.

健康品牌如何在AI搜索中“被看见”？新报告揭示赢家法则

How health and wellness brands win in AI search: new reports reveal the playbook

中NutraIngredients报道，多份行业报告显示，随着越来越多消费者通过AI助手搜索健康与营养建议，品牌在生成式引擎中的可见度（GEO）正成为新的竞争高地。胜出者的共性包括：结构化、可被引用的循证内容，清晰的实体与资质信息，以及在权威第三方平台的稳定存在。对营养健康产业而言，AI搜索优化正从营销加分项变为基础设施，直接影响科学传播与消费决策链路。

ENNutraIngredients reports that as more consumers turn to AI assistants for health and nutrition advice, brand visibility inside generative engines (GEO) is becoming a new competitive battleground. Winners share common traits: structured, citable evidence-based content, clear entity and credential information, and a stable presence on authoritative third-party platforms. For the nutrition industry, AI search optimization is shifting from a marketing bonus to core infrastructure that shapes science communication and consumer decisions.
