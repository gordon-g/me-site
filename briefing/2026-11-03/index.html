FM-04

《自然》：高胆固醇饮食经 Ral–CTSA 通路“拆掉”肝脏 LDL 受体

Nature: high-cholesterol diets dismantle liver LDL receptors via a Ral–CTSA pathway

中加州大学圣迭戈分校在《自然》揭示，高胆固醇饮食激活蛋白 Ral，经组织蛋白酶 A（CTSA）加速肝细胞 LDL 受体降解。抑制 CTSA 后小鼠 LDL 受体更稳定、循环 LDL 显著下降。该通路不同于他汀与 PCSK9 抑制剂，已有通过 I 期安全性试验的抑制剂可候选进入 II 期。

ENUC San Diego scientists reported in Nature that dietary cholesterol activates the protein Ral, which in turn drives cathepsin A (CTSA) to accelerate degradation of LDL receptors on liver cells, eroding the body's ability to clear LDL from the blood. Blocking CTSA with a small molecule stabilised the receptors and sharply lowered circulating LDL in mice. The pathway is distinct from those targeted by statins and PCSK9 inhibitors, and a CTSA inhibitor that already passed a Phase 1 safety trial could move to Phase 2.

《科学》：电化学植入物把多余血糖转成葡萄糖酸盐，小鼠降糖同时减重

Science: an electrochemical implant converts excess glucose into gluconate, cutting sugar and weight

中苏黎世联邦理工 Fussenegger 团队在《科学》报告一款无线供电植入物：石墨毡上的金簇与铂微粒作为无机催化剂，把过量葡萄糖转化为可经尿排出的葡萄糖酸盐，避免多余能量转为脂肪。系统由连续血糖传感器经蓝牙触发，形成闭环。糖尿病小鼠血糖被精准调控并减重，6 周测试稳定、无电热损伤，尚未开展人体试验。

ENETH Zurich's Fussenegger lab reported in Science a wirelessly powered implant whose inorganic catalyst — gold clusters and platinum particles on graphite felt — converts excess glucose into gluconate that the body excretes in urine, keeping surplus energy from being stored as fat. A continuous glucose sensor triggers the device over Bluetooth in a closed loop. In diabetic mice it precisely controlled blood sugar and produced weight loss, ran reliably for six weeks without electrical or thermal tissue damage, and has not yet been tested in humans.
