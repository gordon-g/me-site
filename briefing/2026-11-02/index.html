全食物饮食让克罗恩病高危人群肠道炎症降六成，一级预防首获随机证据

Whole-food diet cuts gut inflammation by 60% in high-risk Crohn's relatives

中沙阿雷泽德克医疗中心的 Dan Turner 团队在《Gastroenterology》发表 PIONIR 随机交叉试验：331 名克罗恩病患者无症状一级亲属中，钙卫蛋白持续升高者接受 8 周 Tasty&Healthy 全食物饮食，钙卫蛋白降 61%，应答率 71%（对照 27%）。

ENIn the PIONIR randomised crossover trial published in Gastroenterology, Dan Turner's team screened 331 asymptomatic first-degree relatives of Crohn's patients and gave an 8-week Tasty&Healthy whole-food diet to those with persistently elevated faecal calprotectin. Calprotectin fell 61% from baseline and 71% responded versus 27% on habitual diet; Coprococcus eutactus enrichment tracked the decline and ultra-processed food intake dropped to zero.

绝经后肠道菌群同步「变老」：短链脂肪酸减少、TMAO 风险放大

Gut microbiome ages with menopause as short-chain fatty acids decline

中科普梳理绝经后菌群与代谢的联动：雌激素下降伴随菌群构成改变，产短链脂肪酸能力（尤其丁酸）减弱、TMAO 通路风险放大、次级胆汁酸代谢改变，进而联动血管僵硬、肝脏脂肪堆积与骨骼肌流失。动物实验显示，补充丁酸或移植正常菌群可减轻卵巢切除小鼠的肝脂变性。

ENA science explainer traces how menopause reshapes the microbiome: falling oestrogen accompanies compositional change, weaker short-chain fatty acid production (especially butyrate), amplified TMAO pathways and altered secondary bile acid metabolism, feeding into arterial stiffness, liver fat accumulation and muscle loss. In ovariectomised mice, butyrate supplementation or healthy microbiota transfer reduced hepatic steatosis.

承重骨骼会「分泌青春」：力学刺激让老年小鼠延寿近 3 个月

Loaded bone secretes rejuvenating factors, extending old mice lifespan

中南方医科大学白晓春团队在《Cell Metabolism》发表：骨细胞 PIEZO1 感知力学负荷，每周两次胫骨动态轴向负荷使老年小鼠寿命延长近 3 个月（约合人类 9 年），多器官状态同步年轻化；骨细胞分泌的 PEDF 单独注射即可复刻效果。尚属小鼠概念验证。

ENIn Cell Metabolism, Bai Xiaochun's team at Southern Medical University reports that osteocyte PIEZO1 senses mechanical load: twice-weekly tibial dynamic axial loading extended old mice lifespan by nearly three months (roughly nine human years) with younger bone, cognition and organ states. PEDF, secreted by osteocytes, reproduced the effect when injected alone and is lower in older adults and osteoporosis patients. Mouse proof-of-concept only.

ENNeurologists argue that stroke prevention should move beyond managing individual risk factors, incorporating biological age and treating stroke as an external sign of dysregulated ageing. Core interventions remain blood pressure control, a Mediterranean diet, 150 minutes of weekly aerobic exercise, six-plus hours of sleep, statins and proper atrial fibrillation treatment. The authors caution that biological age tests and whole-body MRI lack evidence, whereas carotid plaque burden is more reliable.
