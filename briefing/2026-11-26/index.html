38 项 RCT 荟萃：ICU 里「灭菌」与「扶菌」都没降死亡率，肺炎风险却腰斩

Meta-analysis of 38 RCTs: in the ICU neither gut decontamination nor probiotics cut mortality, but pneumonia risk halved

中哥伦比亚大学团队在 Gut Microbes 发表 38 项随机对照试验的荟萃分析，首次并排比较消化道去污染与益生菌、益生元补充两种相反策略。两者均未显著降低 ICU 总体死亡率（相对风险 0.96 与 0.93），但获得性肺炎风险分别降至 0.50 与 0.70，去污染组全因感染降至 0.76。

ENA meta-analysis in Gut Microbes led by Columbia researchers pooled 38 randomized trials and for the first time compared two opposing strategies: selective digestive decontamination versus probiotic and prebiotic supplementation. Neither significantly reduced overall ICU mortality (relative risk 0.96 and 0.93), but acquired pneumonia risk fell to 0.50 and 0.70 respectively, and all-cause infection fell to 0.76 in the decontamination arm.

Nature：一万人的肠道菌，正在跟着宿主一起「变老」

Nature: in 10,000 people, gut bacteria are ageing alongside their hosts

中研究扫描约 1 万人肠道细菌的基因变异，与近 250 项人体特征做关联，发现随年龄出现的特定突变，而与疾病相关的突变很少。其中一项适应似能帮助肠道中丰度最高的菌种之一抵抗随衰老加剧的氧化应激。作者认为菌群会随宿主一同演化，并可能塑造衰老对健康的影响。

ENThe study scanned genetic variation in gut bacteria from about 10,000 people against nearly 250 host traits and found specific mutations that arise with age, while few mutations tracked disease. One adaptation appears to help one of the most abundant gut species withstand oxidative stress, which intensifies with ageing. The authors argue the microbiome evolves alongside its host and may shape how ageing affects health.
