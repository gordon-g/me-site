《新英格兰医学杂志》：炎症性肠病不是一种病，3.5%患者存在抗IL-10自身抗体

NEJM: IBD Is Not One Disease — 3.5% of Patients Carry Antibodies That Neutralize IL-10

中研究分析逾4900名炎症性肠病患者，发现约3.5%体内存在可中和白细胞介素-10的自身抗体，健康对照中未检出；这一自身免疫反应也解释了已知最强遗传风险位点如何致病。作者认为IBD实为多种生物学机制不同的疾病集合，应按病因分层治疗，英国估计1.5万至2万人携带此类抗体。

ENAnalyzing more than 4,900 people with IBD, researchers found about 3.5% carry antibodies that neutralize interleukin-10, with none detected in healthy controls; the autoimmune reaction also explains how the strongest known genetic risk factor drives disease. The authors argue IBD is really a set of biologically distinct conditions that should be treated by cause, with an estimated 15,000 to 20,000 patients in the UK carrying these antibodies.

Same Protein, Different Outcome: Beef Worsens and Pea Protein Eases Gut Inflammation in Mice

中美国胃肠病学协会旗下《细胞与分子胃肠病学》研究，在多种IBD小鼠模型中比较牛肉、鸡蛋、乳、大豆、豌豆五种蛋白配方：牛肉蛋白组结肠炎最重，豌豆蛋白组仅轻度炎症，雌雄与不同模型结果一致；未诱导结肠炎时单喂牛肉蛋白并不致病，提示菌群、肠屏障与胆汁酸是中间环节。

给肠道「大扫除」：抗菌肽pileup被激活后可强制老废肠细胞脱落

Spring-Cleaning the Gut: Antimicrobial Peptide pileup Forces Aged Intestinal Cells to Shed

中《Current Biology》果蝇研究鉴定出抗菌肽pileup：肠细胞线粒体老化、活性氧升高时，HIF-1α经ATG1被激活并指令老化细胞表达pileup，后者破坏细胞膜并触发JNK通路促其脱落。敲除pileup及其3个同源基因后，肠道堆积肿瘤样细胞、屏障崩溃、果蝇显著早亡，提示抗菌肽还承担内部「保洁」功能。

ENA Current Biology study in fruit flies identified the antimicrobial peptide pileup: when intestinal cells suffer mitochondrial decline and rising reactive oxygen species, HIF-1α is activated via ATG1 and instructs aged cells to express pileup, which disrupts their membranes and triggers JNK-driven shedding. Knocking out pileup and its three homologs caused tumor-like cell accumulation, barrier collapse and early death, suggesting antimicrobial peptides also serve as internal janitors.

中夏威夷大学团队分析123名成人的粪便与血液样本，发现肠道菌群组成可解释约15%的生物学衰老速度差异（DunedinPACE指标），且独立于实际年龄。青春双歧杆菌与较慢衰老关联最强，解糖琥珀酸弧菌则与加速衰老相关，提示菌群可作为精准健康与长寿干预的可调控靶点。

中新疆以「1+1+1」「N+1+1」家庭医生团队模式，把县级专科专家、乡镇医护与村医编组，建立二三级医院医师常态化下沉包联机制；签约居民获动态电子健康档案、慢病随访、长处方与转诊绿色通道，新就业群体逐步纳入。拜城县还把健康服务嵌入村公共卫生委员会与基层治理网络，破解随访找不到人、群众不配合的难题。

ENXinjiang is organizing family doctor teams on a 1+1+1 and N+1+1 model that pairs county specialists, township clinicians and village doctors, with a standing mechanism for secondary and tertiary hospital physicians to be assigned to grassroots units. Contracted residents receive dynamically updated electronic health records, chronic disease follow-up, long prescriptions and green-channel referrals, with gig workers gradually included; Baicheng County embeds services in village public health committees to solve follow-up and non-cooperation problems.

杭州萧山试点「萧慧减」体重管理智能体：135人30天人均减重3.5公斤

Hangzhou's Xiaoshan Pilots AI Weight Agent: 3.5 kg Lost in 30 Days Across 135 Users

中杭州萧山区卫健局在萧山医院试点上线「萧慧减」体重管理智能体，以「AI+医生」打通筛查评估、方案制定与执行跟踪全周期：报告关键指标识别准确率与召回率均达99%，方案须医生审核后执行。试运行135名真实用户30天人均减重3.5公斤，食物AI识别准确率90.2%，并在33名学生中开展儿童体重干预试点。

ENXiaoshan District's health commission launched the Xiaohuijian weight management agent at Xiaoshan Hospital, using an AI-plus-doctor model across screening, assessment, planning and follow-up: report recognition reaches 99% precision and recall, and every plan requires physician sign-off. In a pilot with 135 real users, average weight loss was 3.5 kg over 30 days, food image recognition hit 90.2% accuracy, and a childhood weight program now covers 33 students.
