《细胞》：肠道菌把「硝酸盐＋铁」合成 DNIC，为心脏代谢撑起保护伞

Cell: Gut bacteria turn dietary nitrate plus iron into DNIC to protect cardiometabolic health

中瑞典卡罗林斯卡医学院在《细胞》报道：肠道细菌借硝酸盐还原酶，用饮食中的硝酸盐与非血红素铁合成二亚硝酰铁配合物（DNIC）。该分子半衰期逾 3 小时，随血抵达心、肝、肾，激活 sGC-cGMP 并抑制 mTORC1-SREBP1。西式饮食模型小鼠干预 8 周后血压、心脏结构、血管功能、血糖血脂与脂肪肝均改善；作者提醒勿自行大剂量补铁或硝酸盐。

ENKarolinska Institutet researchers reported in Cell that gut bacteria use nitrate reductase to build dinitrosyl iron complexes (DNIC) from dietary nitrate and non-heme iron. Stable for over three hours, DNIC travels to heart, liver and kidney, activating sGC-cGMP while suppressing mTORC1-SREBP1. Eight weeks of intervention improved blood pressure, cardiac structure, vascular function, glucose, lipids and fatty liver in mice on a Western diet; the authors caution against self-dosing iron or nitrate supplements.

ENA Monash University team reported in Immunity that granzyme K (GzmK), upregulated in aged memory CD8+ T cells, drives age-related tissue decline. GzmK-knockout mice retained immune-ageing phenotypes yet showed markedly less organ ageing: less renal complement deposition and improved liver function with higher decorin and weaker TGF-beta signalling. The work separates organismal from immune ageing and nominates GzmK as a target for healthier ageing without compromising immunity.

中第 20 个联合国糖尿病日，上海公布慢病防控数据：截至 2026 年，全市建档在册血糖风险人群累计 257.4 万人，已接受糖尿病筛查者 114.1 万人；年内新登记糖尿病患者 10.8 万人，社区随访在管患者 101.2 万人。全市 231 家社区卫生服务中心设立慢病健康管理支持中心，可开展综合风险评估、筛查、智能随访与标准化血压血糖测量。

ENMarking the 20th UN Diabetes Day, Shanghai released chronic disease figures: by 2026 the city had registered 2.574 million people at high blood-glucose risk, with 1.141 million screened; 108,000 new diabetes cases were recorded during the year and 1.012 million patients were under community follow-up. A total of 231 community health centres now host chronic disease management support centres offering risk assessment, screening, smart follow-up and standardised blood pressure and glucose measurement.

国家卫健委提示理性减重：95% 以上三级公立综合医院已设体重管理门诊

NHC urges sensible weight loss as over 95% of tertiary hospitals open weight clinics

中针对部分减肥训练营以「急速暴瘦」「不瘦退款」招揽学员、靠过度节食与超负荷训练减重并多次发生伤亡，国家卫健委提示：体重不是越轻越好，过度节食与超负荷运动可致脱水、脱发、电解质紊乱与肌肉流失；减重前应做医学与运动风险评估，出现胸闷心悸头晕须立即停止；目前超过 95% 的三级公立综合医院已设置健康体重管理门诊。

ENResponding to weight-loss camps advertising rapid results and money-back promises built on extreme dieting and overtraining, some ending in deaths, the National Health Commission warns that lighter is not healthier: crash dieting and excessive exercise can cause dehydration, hair loss, electrolyte imbalance and muscle loss. It advises medical and exercise risk assessment before any programme, immediate stopping if chest tightness, palpitations or dizziness occur, and notes that over 95% of tertiary public general hospitals now run healthy weight management clinics.

个人医保云落地慢病管理：4.5 亿患者有了「出院后盯护」

Personal medical-insurance cloud brings post-discharge AI monitoring to 450 million patients

中湖南湘江新区企业基于国家级「医保云」推出慢病全周期方案：整合医保结算、院内报告、用药明细与家用血压计、血糖仪数据，生成动态健康画像；居家监测异常自动触发复诊预警，家庭医生收到随访任务。方案依托覆盖 3.5 万余个病种、3550 万医学实体的知识图谱与多模态大模型，其肾慢病路径获 2026 全国智慧医保大赛一等奖。我国慢病患者约 4.5 亿，慢病死亡占居民总死亡 88%。

ENA Hunan Xiangjiang New Area company has built a full-cycle chronic disease programme on China's national personal medical-insurance cloud, merging claims records, hospital reports, medication details and home blood pressure and glucose readings into a live health profile. Abnormal home readings trigger revisit alerts while family doctors receive follow-up tasks. The system relies on a knowledge graph covering more than 35,000 diseases and 35.5 million medical entities plus a multimodal model; its kidney-disease pathway won first prize at the 2026 national smart insurance competition. China has about 450 million chronic disease patients, and such diseases account for 88% of deaths.
