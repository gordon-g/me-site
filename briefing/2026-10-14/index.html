Nature子刊观点：短链脂肪酸的肠道释放“地理学”，决定降压还是升压

Nature journal viewpoint: the gut 'geography' of short-chain fatty acid release decides blood pressure effects

中一篇发表于Nature子刊的观点文章提出，膳食纤维经菌群发酵生成的短链脂肪酸（SCFA）对血压的影响取决于其在肠道不同部位的释放位置：近端结肠高浓度SCFA经特定感觉神经与内分泌通路可能产生降压效应，而远端或循环中模式不同则可能参与升压调节。作者呼吁未来研究应关注SCFA产生的“空间地理学”，而非仅测量总量，这为膳食纤维与降压营养干预的个体化设计提供了新视角。

ENA viewpoint article in a Nature journal argues that the blood-pressure impact of microbially derived short-chain fatty acids depends on where along the intestine they are released: high luminal SCFA concentrations in proximal colon engage distinct neural and endocrine circuits that may lower blood pressure, whereas distal or systemic patterns can contribute to pressor effects. The authors call for mapping the spatial 'geography' of SCFA production rather than measuring totals alone, informing individualized fiber-based interventions.

The authors suggest that raising spermidine through everyday diet could be a low-cost nutritional strategy for exercise capacity in aging cardiovascular patients, pending intervention trials.

肠道菌群决定你实际吸收多少热量：DAMM模型给出首个定量框架

Gut microbes decide how many calories you absorb: DAMM model offers first quantitative framework

中亚利桑那州立大学团队在PLOS One发表DAMM（消化、吸收与微生物代谢）模型，首次将肠道菌群纳入热量计算：约85%的能量在上消化道被人体吸收，其余15%进入结肠由菌群发酵，人体平均每日从结肠吸收的短链脂肪酸约140千卡、占总可用能量7.4%。对照饮食试验显示，高纤维“促菌群饮食”比典型西式饮食日均少吸收约116千卡且饥饿感未增，提示食物标签热量并非人人相同，为个性化营养与体重管理提供新工具。

ENArizona State University researchers present in PLOS One the DAMM (Digestion, Absorption and Microbial Metabolism) model, the first to fold gut microbes into calorie accounting: roughly 85% of energy is absorbed in the upper gut while 15% reaches the colon for microbial fermentation, with colon-derived short-chain fatty acids contributing about 140 kcal per day, or 7.4% of usable energy. In a controlled crossover trial, a high-fiber microbiome-enhancer diet yielded about 116 fewer absorbed calories daily than a Western diet without increased hunger, challenging one-size-fits-all calorie labels.

ENCFi.CN reports that RAY1225, a novel GLP-1 drug developed by a subsidiary of China's Zhongsheng Pharma, has delivered positive Phase III topline results (REBUILDING-2) in overweight and obese adults, meeting efficacy and safety expectations and outperforming benchmarks. Its biweekly dosing and long-acting profile are seen as supporting potential out-licensing. Domestic obesity drugs are moving from proof of concept to value delivery, broadening access for weight management in China.

STEP UP事后分析：司美格鲁肽72周使平均肝脂从8.8%降至3.1%

STEP UP post hoc: semaglutide cut mean liver fat from 8.8% to 3.1% over 72 weeks

中Pharmacy Times报道，诺和诺德在EASD 2026公布的STEP UP事后分析显示，肥胖成人接受司美格鲁肽72周后平均肝脂从8.8%降至3.1%，26名肝脂高于5%阈值的影像学亚组受试者中23人降至阈值以下。结果为探索性、样本小且无安慰剂对照，但支持正在进行的ESSENCE III期试验——检验司美格鲁肽治疗MASH合并肝纤维化，进一步拓展GLP-1在代谢健康管理中的版图。

ENPharmacy Times reports a post hoc analysis of Novo Nordisk's STEP UP and STEP UP T2D trials presented at EASD 2026: after 72 weeks of semaglutide, mean liver fat in adults with obesity fell from 8.8% to 3.1%, and 23 of 26 imaging-subgroup participants above the 5% threshold dropped below it. The findings are exploratory, small and placebo-uncontrolled, but support the ongoing Phase III ESSENCE trial of semaglutide in MASH with fibrosis, extending GLP-1's metabolic health footprint.

仅7次力量训练即可缩小脂肪细胞：小鼠实验重启燃脂分子通路

Just seven strength sessions shrink fat cells: mice study reactivates fat-burning pathways

中ScienceDaily报道的一项研究发现，肥胖小鼠仅进行7次抗阻训练后，尽管总体重未见变化，内脏脂肪细胞已显著缩小，与分解储存脂肪相关的分子通路被重新激活。研究者指出，这一“体重不变但脂肪减少”的现象提示力量训练的健康价值可能被体重秤低估，对于体重管理中的“减脂保肌”目标，规律抗阻运动的价值应获得与有氧运动同等的重视。

ENA study covered by ScienceDaily found that after just seven resistance-training sessions, obese mice showed significantly smaller visceral fat cells and reactivated fat-mobilization molecular pathways despite no change in body weight. The 'same scale weight, less fat' effect suggests strength training's value is underestimated by the scale, and for fat loss with muscle preservation, regular resistance exercise deserves parity with aerobic training in weight-management programs.
