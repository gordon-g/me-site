《代谢》：肠道菌群先于宿主「变老」，代偿耗竭即触发全身代谢过载

Metabolism: the gut microbiome ages ahead of its host, and exhausted compensation triggers systemic metabolic overload

中研究提出「代谢生物学年龄差值」量化长期累积的稳态负荷，发现中年阶段存在宿主与菌群的衰老时差：微生物功能衰退早于全身衰老的临床表现。一旦稳态负荷越过代偿临界点，1,3,7-三甲基尿酸富集、全反式维甲酸耗竭等特征代谢物变化便推动系统性衰老，提示体检正常不等于菌群未老。

ENThe study proposes a metabolic biological-age gap to quantify cumulative allostatic load, revealing a host-microbiome ageing mismatch in midlife: microbial functional decline precedes clinical signs of systemic ageing. Once load crosses the compensation threshold, metabolite shifts such as 1,3,7-trimethyluric acid accumulation and all-trans-retinoic acid depletion drive systemic ageing, so a normal check-up does not mean an unaged microbiome.

中团队锁定编码 5-oxoETE 合成酶的基因 DHRS7 及其受体 OXER1，发现这条经典「招募免疫细胞」的警报通路同时保护邻近健康细胞免受免疫反应产生的化学应激，机制依赖 NUDIX 水解酶。抑制该通路反而加重炎症；炎症性肠病患者肠组织 5-oxoETE 偏低与更差结局相关。

ENResearchers identified DHRS7 as the gene encoding the 5-oxoETE synthase and OXER1 as its receptor, showing that this classic immune-cell recruiting alarm also shields neighbouring healthy cells from the chemical stress of the immune response, via NUDIX hydrolases. Blocking the pathway worsens inflammation, and low intestinal 5-oxoETE in IBD tracks with poorer outcomes.

《自然-代谢》：全血 NAD 不随年龄下降，为抗衰而补 NMN 缺乏依据

Nature Metabolism: whole-blood NAD does not fall with age, leaving little basis for NMN for longevity

中立陶宛团队对 1068 人完成 1340 次全血 NAD 检测，发现健康人 NAD 不随年龄下降，但心血管疾病与高血压患者显著下降。每日口服 1000 mg NMN 可把 NAD 从约 19–20 μM 推高到 51–56 μM，半衰期 3.2–4.3 天，远超生理范围，长期安全性未知。

ENA Lithuanian team ran 1,340 whole-blood NAD measurements in 1,068 adults and found NAD does not fall with age in healthy people (flat in men, rising slightly in women), yet declines markedly in cardiovascular disease and hypertension. Daily 1,000 mg NMN raised NAD from about 19-20 microM to 51-56 microM with a 3.2-4.3 day half-life, far above physiological range, with unknown long-term safety.
