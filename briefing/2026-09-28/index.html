中研究对59名慢性收缩性心衰患者与50名健康人做深度多组学分析，发现心衰患者菌群多样性显著降低，产短链脂肪酸的双歧杆菌等有益菌耗竭，碳水化合物发酵通路全面下调，转向促炎的氧化型代谢；色氨酸代谢物吲哚-3-丙酸在患者血中大幅下降且与病情严重度相关。体外实验证实多株双歧杆菌可合成该护心物质，仅凭菌群代谢通路特征区分心衰与健康的曲线下面积达0.94。

ENDeep multi-omics analysis of 59 chronic heart-failure patients and 50 healthy controls found markedly reduced microbial diversity, depletion of SCFA-producing bifidobacteria, a shift toward pro-inflammatory oxidative metabolism, and sharply lower blood levels of the cardioprotective tryptophan metabolite indole-3-propionic acid, correlating with disease severity. In vitro work confirmed several Bifidobacterium strains can synthesize it, and microbial functional features alone distinguished patients from controls with an AUC of 0.94.
