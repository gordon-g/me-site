Holiday Metabolism Guide: Skip the Scale Obsession, Build Micro-habits to Protect Muscle and Hormones

中上海健康播报发布假期体重管理提示：肌脂均衡与激素稳定才是核心，不必紧盯体重秤。饮食上固定进餐顺序——先蔬菜、再优质蛋白、后主食，聚餐七分饱、饮料换茶水；运动上每天10-15分钟徒手深蹲、臀桥等居家力量即可保肌，旅行每天累计6000-8000步；不建议节后立刻长时间高强度有氧，易致肌肉流失；体重涨1-2公斤多为水分滞留，维持习惯即可回落。

ENShanghai health authorities advise focusing on muscle-fat balance and hormonal stability rather than scale weight over the holiday: fix meal order (vegetables first, then protein, staples last), stop at 70% fullness, and swap sugary drinks for tea. Ten to fifteen minutes of daily bodyweight strength protects muscle; 6,000-8,000 steps while traveling suffices. A 1-2 kg post-holiday rise is mostly water, so avoid punishing post-holiday endurance sessions that cost muscle.

英国率先确立AI医疗器械全生命周期监测规则，MHRA从一次性审批转向持续监管

UK Pioneers Lifecycle Monitoring Rules for AI Medical Devices as MHRA Moves Beyond One-off Approval

中英国药品与健康产品管理局（MHRA）负责人10月6日表示：基于AI的医疗器械获批后仍需持续监测，而非一次性上市前安全审查；政府将接受国家AI医疗委员会全部44项建议，2026年底前出草案、2027年春发布完整实施计划，覆盖NHS落地目标。MHRA同期启动AI Airlock第三期，聚焦上市后监测与生命周期管理，多国正就这套全球最全面规则赴英取经。

ENThe UK's MHRA said on Oct 6 that AI-based medical devices will need constant post-market monitoring rather than a single pre-approval check. The government accepted all 44 recommendations of its AI-in-healthcare commission, with a draft plan by end-2026 and full implementation by spring 2027 across the NHS; the AI Airlock sandbox enters phase three focused on surveillance and lifecycle oversight, drawing interest from regulators worldwide.
