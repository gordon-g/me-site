《自然综述·微生物学》：被忽视的小肠菌群，或是下一代活菌药的富矿

Nature Reviews Microbiology: the overlooked small-intestine microbiome as a frontier for live biotherapeutics

中综述指出，小肠靠 2—5 小时快速转运、胆汁冲刷与相对高氧，养出以链球菌、韦荣球菌为主的特有菌群。双酶梭菌上调 DGAT2 促甘油三酯合成；乳酸菌苯乳酸经 PPARγ 减轻高脂与抗生素暴露小鼠增重；代谢物还刺激肠内分泌细胞释放 5-HT 与 GLP-1。作者称无创定位采样仍是活菌药落地的瓶颈。

ENThe review notes that the small intestine's 2–5 hour transit, bile wash and relatively high oxygen shape a distinct community dominated by Streptococcus and Veillonella. Clostridium symbiosum upregulates DGAT2 to drive triglyceride synthesis; Lactobacillus-derived phenyllactic acid acts via PPARγ to limit weight gain in mice on high-fat diets or antibiotics; metabolites also trigger enteroendocrine release of serotonin and GLP-1. Non-invasive site-specific sampling remains the bottleneck for live biotherapeutics.

ENAnalyzing 72 colorectal cancer specimens, Ruijin Hospital researchers found that tumors rich in tertiary lymphoid structures harbor more Escherichia–Shigella, with bacteria sitting inside T- and B-cell aggregates. Oral Shigella flexneri induced these structures and suppressed tumors in orthotopic and spontaneous mouse models by activating type I interferon, prompting dendritic cells to release CCL19/CXCL10 and follicular helper T cells to release CXCL13; depleting dendritic cells abolished the effect.

中一项发表于《科学》的分析把人类寿命的遗传率从此前估计的 20%—25% 上调到约 55%，意味着基因对寿命的影响长期被低估。研究者认为余下约 45% 由生活方式、医疗可及性、社会经济地位乃至细胞分裂的随机性决定，这为在痴呆与心血管病发生前做遗传分层与早期干预提供了依据，但强调遗传并非宿命。

ENAn analysis in Science raises the heritability of human lifespan from earlier estimates of 20–25% to roughly 55%, suggesting genetics has long been underestimated. The remaining ~45% is attributed to lifestyle, access to care, socioeconomic status and even randomness in cell division. The authors say this supports genetic stratification and earlier intervention before dementia or cardiovascular disease appears, while stressing that genes are not destiny.

LM-02

MIT《自然·衰老》：老年人深睡时「洗脑」的脑脊液波明显变弱

MIT in Nature Aging: brain-washing cerebrospinal fluid waves weaken with age during deep sleep

中MIT 团队比较 18—39 岁与 60—84 岁受试者夜间约两小时的 fMRI 与脑电：年轻人进入非快速眼动睡眠后慢波增强、脑脊液波随之变大；老年人平均脑脊液波显著更小，且与慢波减弱相关，并非只是睡得少。神经血管耦合与屏气脑血流反应下降可解释部分差异，灰质萎缩越明显者降幅越大。

ENMIT researchers compared adults aged 18–39 and 60–84 during roughly two-hour nighttime fMRI and EEG sessions. In younger adults, non-REM slow waves strengthened and cerebrospinal fluid waves grew; in older adults CSF waves were significantly smaller, tied to weaker slow waves rather than simply less sleep. Reduced neurovascular coupling and blunted breath-hold blood-flow responses explained part of the gap, and greater gray-matter atrophy tracked with larger declines, though some older adults retained youthful flow.

石家庄家医「这一年」：4331 支团队，并用声纹筛查老人认知障碍

Shijiazhuang reviews a year of family doctors: 4,331 teams and voice-based cognitive screening

中11 月 14 日，石家庄市卫健委举办「家庭医生签约服务—我们这一年」活动。全市组建 4331 支「四位一体」家医团队，并在楼宇公示团队信息。现场启动主动健康试点：即日起至明年 1 月 20 日，在 4 家社区卫生服务中心用声纹技术为 65 岁以上老人做认知障碍快筛，2027 年扩至 20 家。

ENOn Nov 14 Shijiazhuang's health commission held a review event for family doctor contracting. The city has divided service grids and formed 4,331 four-in-one family doctor teams, fixed the second Saturday of each month as a community visiting day and posted team details in residential buildings. An active-health pilot also launched: from now to Jan 20 next year, four community health centers will use voiceprint technology to screen and intervene for cognitive impairment in residents over 65, expanding to 20 centers in 2027.

国家卫健委发布会：健康素养 33.69%，「0 蔗糖」「零添加」不等于更健康

NHC briefing: health literacy at 33.69%, and zero sucrose or no additives does not mean healthier

中11 月 13 日国家卫健委发布会聚焦立冬健康与健康素养：我国居民健康素养 2025 年为 33.69%，距 2030 年 40% 目标仍有差距。专家提醒「0 蔗糖」只针对蔗糖、「零添加」不等于低糖低盐；新标签标准强制标示饱和脂肪与糖，2027 年 3 月 16 日起禁用「零添加」等用语。

ENA Nov 13 National Health Commission briefing focused on winter health and health literacy, which reached 33.69% in 2025, still short of the 40% target for 2030. Experts warned that zero sucrose covers only sucrose, no additives does not mean low sugar or salt, and non-fried does not mean low fat; the new labeling standard mandates saturated fat and sugar declarations and bans no-additive claims from March 16, 2027. COPD and diabetes day themes stress early diagnosis and early action.
