ENThe RESET for Remission trial, published in the Lancet Regional Health Americas and presented at EASD 2026: 96 adults with early-onset type 2 diabetes and obesity were randomized to 24 weeks of an 800-900 kcal/day low-energy diet plus supervised aerobic and resistance exercise. Remission (HbA1c below 6.5% off glucose-lowering drugs for 12 weeks) was achieved by 54% versus 4% on usual care, with 8.4 kg mean weight loss, preserved muscle mass, better fitness and cardiovascular measures, and no serious adverse events.

ScienceDaily: two-year RCT finds diet drinks match water for weight loss, probing sweetener doubts

中一项纳入493名超重或肥胖成人的两年随机对照试验显示：以阿斯巴甜、安赛蜜或三氯蔗糖甜化的无糖软饮料在长期减重与维持上与水效果基本相当，两组体重、腰围、体成分及多项代谢指标改善幅度接近。结果挑战“代糖干扰代谢”的部分担忧，但研究者提示仍需结合个体反应与整体膳食质量解读，不宜简单化推广。

ENA two-year randomized trial in 493 adults with overweight or obesity found that diet soft drinks sweetened with aspartame, acesulfame-K or sucralose were essentially as effective as water for long-term weight loss and maintenance, with similar improvements in weight, waist size, body composition and several metabolic measures in both groups. The results temper some concerns that non-nutritive sweeteners derail metabolism, though researchers caution that individual responses and overall diet quality still matter.
