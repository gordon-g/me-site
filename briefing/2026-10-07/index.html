UCAS team engineers gut bacteria to repair the gut-brain axis, easing colitis and psychiatric comorbidity

MIT team in Nature: ketogenic diet drives small-intestinal tumors via lipids, not ketones, while suppressing colonic tumors

中ScienceDaily 10月5日报道，MIT团队发表于Nature的研究显示：生酮饮食使易患肠癌小鼠的小肠肿瘤增多，却令结肠肿瘤减少，同一饮食在相邻肠段作用截然相反。机制上风险并非来自酮体，而是肠道干细胞燃烧高脂脂肪酸氧化、激活PPAR家族加速增殖；家族性腺瘤性息肉病等遗传易感人群尤需警惕，商用酮酯补剂不会重现两类效应，生酮饮食的肠段特异性风险亟待人群研究厘清。

ENScienceDaily reported October 5 on an MIT study in Nature: a ketogenic diet increased small-intestinal tumors in cancer-prone mice while reducing colonic tumors, with opposite effects in adjacent gut segments. The risk traced not to ketones but to intestinal stem cells oxidizing the diet's high fat load, activating PPAR proteins and accelerating proliferation. People with inherited predispositions such as familial adenomatous polyposis should take note, while commercial ketone supplements would not reproduce either effect.

中据PR Newswire 10月7日消息，剑桥大学孵化的Cambridge Heartwear获英国NHS供应链1.84亿英镑采购，其HeartSense可穿戴集诊断级多导联心电、自适应AI与无线充电于一身，患者居家即可持续监测心律，更早识别心衰与卒中风险；产品已获UKCA与ISO 13485认证、FDA 510(k)申请进行中，兼顾女性心血管人体工学，将向初级与二级医疗体系规模部署。

ENPR Newswire reported October 7 that Cambridge Heartwear, spun out of the University of Cambridge, won a £184 million UK NHS Supply Chain tender for HeartSense, the first medical-grade wearable combining diagnostic multichannel ECG, adaptive AI and wireless charging, letting patients monitor heart rhythm at home to catch heart failure and stroke risk earlier. UKCA and ISO 13485 certified with FDA 510(k) pending, the device features ergonomic design for women's cardiovascular care and will scale across primary and secondary care.

ScienceDaily：产后护理加配患者导航员，抑郁筛查与长期随访显著改善

ScienceDaily: adding patient navigators to postpartum care markedly improves depression screening and follow-up

中ScienceDaily 10月6日报道，为新手妈妈配备患者导航员可显著提升产后照护完成率：受试母亲更可能获得产后访视、抑郁筛查、预防性检测与持续初级保健连接，导航员通过协助解决交通、托育、预约与复杂医疗信息等现实障碍发挥作用。研究者认为这一低成本增量服务有望成为改善女性长期健康的有力杠杆，为产后抑郁高发背景下的健康管理服务设计提供可复制样板。

ENScienceDaily reported October 6 that pairing new mothers with patient navigators markedly improved postpartum care completion: participants were far more likely to receive postpartum visits, depression screening, preventive testing and ongoing primary care, as navigators helped clear practical barriers such as transportation, childcare, scheduling and confusing medical information. Researchers see this low-cost add-on as a powerful lever for women's long-term health and a replicable template for postpartum health-management services.
