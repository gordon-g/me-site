Who Really Needs a Coronary Calcium Scan? Ten-Year Study of 6,000+ Adults: Limited Value for Many, Greatest Value at Borderline Risk

中9月12日报道的一项覆盖6000多名成年人、随访10年的研究发现，热门的冠状动脉钙化（CAC）扫描对许多人的标准心脏病风险评估几乎没有额外价值；但对于处于边缘或中等风险的患者，该扫描可能显著改变风险分层与干预决策。研究提示CAC筛查应更精准地瞄准'不确定地带'人群，而非无差别推广。

ENA ten-year study reported September 12 covering more than 6,000 adults found that the popular coronary artery calcium (CAC) scan adds little to standard heart disease risk assessment for many people — but for patients at borderline or intermediate risk it may meaningfully change risk stratification and intervention decisions, suggesting CAC screening should be targeted at the 'uncertain zone' population rather than promoted indiscriminately.
