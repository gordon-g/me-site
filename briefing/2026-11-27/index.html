《自然》：肠道 IgA 是抑癌「刹车」，缺失时结肠肿瘤翻倍

Nature: Gut IgA Acts as a Cancer Brake — Without It, Colon Tumors Double

中洛克菲勒大学在《自然》报告，缺失 IgA 的小鼠结肠肿瘤数量是正常小鼠的两倍以上，且在多种肠癌模型中都成立。机制上 IgA 结合肠上皮 DMBT1 受体，给细菌驱动的上皮增殖踩刹车；对缺抗体小鼠口服 IgA 后，升高的肿瘤风险消失。人类 IgA 缺乏约每 500 人 1 例，与结直肠癌风险升高相关。

ENRockefeller University reports in Nature that mice lacking immunoglobulin A developed more than twice as many colon tumors as normal mice, across several cancer models. IgA binds the epithelial receptor DMBT1 and restrains bacteria-driven cell proliferation; oral IgA erased the excess risk. IgA deficiency affects about 1 in 500 people in the US and is linked to higher colorectal cancer risk.

中科罗拉多大学发现肾脏蛋白 TMEM174 通过结合 NPT2A，限制约 75% 已滤过磷酸盐被重新吸收入血，相当于天然刹车。动物缺失该蛋白后血磷升高、血管钙化加速、寿命显著缩短，高磷饮食下更明显；减少膳食磷酸盐可部分减轻损伤。作者称血磷浓度越高，哺乳动物寿命越短。

ENScientists at Colorado identified kidney protein TMEM174 as a natural brake that binds NPT2A and limits reabsorption of roughly 75% of filtered phosphate. The senior author says the higher the blood phosphate, the shorter the lifespan.
