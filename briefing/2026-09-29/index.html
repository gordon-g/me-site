唯蜜瘦智能腹部按摩仪多中心临床研究于《中医临床研究》刊发

Multicenter study of Weimisou smart abdominal massager published in TCM Clinical Research

中由浙江中医药大学、北京中医药大学王琦书院、国家中医体质与治未病研究院等共同完成的多中心开放标签单臂试验发表于《中医临床研究》：61名超重及肥胖受试者使用神阙穴温灸联合EMS脉冲的智能腹部按摩仪并配合饮食干预2个月，平均减重10.8kg、腰围缩减13.6cm、BMI下降4.1，全部受试者减重≥5%。研究为消费级中医体重管理设备提供循证支撑，但缺乏安慰剂对照，解读需谨慎。

ENA multicenter, open-label, single-arm trial published in TCM Clinical Research, conducted with Zhejiang Chinese Medical University and Beijing University of Chinese Medicine, enrolled 61 adults with overweight or obesity. Two months of a smart abdominal massager combining Shenque-point moxibustion heating with EMS pulses plus dietary intervention yielded mean weight loss of 10.8 kg, waist reduction of 13.6 cm and BMI drop of 4.1; all participants lost ≥5%. Lacking placebo control, results warrant cautious interpretation.

武汉市卫健委回应政协提案：将分层推进餐后2小时血糖纳入体检筛查

Wuhan health commission to stage in 2-hour postprandial glucose screening in checkups

中武汉市卫健委9月28日答复市政协提案称，我国糖尿病患病率超12%，30%-50%人群仅靠空腹血糖筛查会发生漏诊，餐后2小时血糖是识别糖耐量减低与隐匿糖尿病的核心指标。受检测流程、场地人力与成本约束，暂不宜将全员强制列为体检基础项目，将按「高危人群优先纳入、分层设置套餐、标准化流程、全域科普宣教」分步推进，落实糖尿病防治行动实施方案要求。

ENReplying to a CPPCC proposal on Sept 28, the Wuhan Health Commission noted diabetes prevalence exceeds 12% and 30-50% of people are missed by fasting-glucose-only screening; 2-hour postprandial glucose is key to identifying impaired glucose tolerance and occult diabetes. Citing workflow and cost constraints, it will proceed in stages — high-risk groups first, tiered checkup packages, standardized procedures and public education — per the national diabetes prevention and control action plan.
