Gut Microbes：肠道代谢物吲哚-3-丙酸选择性抑制肝糖异生

Gut Microbes: Gut metabolite indole-3-propionate selectively suppresses hepatic gluconeogenesis

中希伯来大学 Sharabi 团队在 Gut Microbes 报告：色氨酸菌群代谢物吲哚-3-丙酸（IPA）选择性抑制丙酮酸/乳酸与谷氨酰胺驱动的肝糖异生，保留甘油通路。13C 示踪示其降低碳源进入糖异生与 TCA 循环，改变复合体 I 依赖的 NAD⁺/NADH 平衡，改善西方饮食小鼠空腹血糖。

ENHebrew University's Sharabi group reported in Gut Microbes that the tryptophan metabolite indole-3-propionate (IPA) selectively blunts gluconeogenesis from pyruvate/lactate and glutamine while sparing glycerol. 13C tracing showed less carbon entering gluconeogenesis and the TCA cycle, altered complex I-dependent NAD+/NADH balance and suppressed CPS1 urea cycling, improving fasting glucose in Western-diet mice.
