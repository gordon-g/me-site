ENCCTV reports that the same person can register wildly different body-fat readings on two consumer scales (37.1% vs 24% in one test). Experts explain these devices don't measure fat directly: they take bioelectrical impedance and estimate body fat via each brand's proprietary algorithm, so results vary. The advice: use a body-fat scale as a trend tool, watching two-to-four-week direction rather than absolute single readings, and ground health decisions in waist circumference and blood markers.

China Salt Reduction Week: hidden salt in noodles and shrinking labels deserve scrutiny

中每年9月15日所在周为中国减盐周，“915”寓意“就要5克”。我国成人日均盐摄入约9.3克，远超5克建议量，高盐升高血压并增加心脑血管病、肾病、骨质疏松、胃癌和肥胖风险。专家提醒警惕挂面、面包等隐形高盐食品与营养成分表的“缩水”标注，善用限盐工具、外卖备注少盐、逐步减量，慢病患者每日建议不超3克。

ENThe week of September 15 is China's Salt Reduction Week — "915" punning on "just 5 grams." Chinese adults average about 9.3 grams of salt daily, nearly double the 5-gram recommendation, raising risks of hypertension, cardiovascular and kidney disease, osteoporosis, gastric cancer and obesity. Experts urge watching hidden salt in dried noodles and bread, checking nutrition labels, using salt-limiting tools, requesting less salt in takeout and cutting back gradually; chronic-disease patients should stay under 3 grams a day.
