ABOUT

About me

I have spent years working on functional medicine and precision nutrition — both the research and its delivery, with a focus on nutrigenomics, healthy longevity and individualized nutrition intervention. I am also an AI tool builder: I like taking the dirty, repetitive work I meet in research and turning it into a tool that runs by itself.

Over the years I moved from “making plans” to “building tools”, and brought what I learned from building back into planning. I believe good health content should be evidence-based, traceable and free of fluff — that is the bottom line for everything I do.

If you have a health question you cannot quite place, or a workflow that repeats itself maddeningly, let's talk.
